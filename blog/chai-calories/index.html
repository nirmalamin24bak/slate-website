Your chai over a day: what 3 or 4 cups add up to

By Nirmal Amin, Sonal Systems. Published 11 October 2026, updated 14 October 2026.

Four cups of home chai a day, each with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar, add up to about 245 calories. The same four cups without sugar are about 115. One cup is about 60; the masala chai page has the per-cup arithmetic. This post adds up the day: cups, spoons of sugar, milk and cutting chai.

Key takeaways

  • Four cups with 2 teaspoons of sugar each is about 245 calories a day; without sugar, about 115.
  • Sugar is the biggest part of the day's number: each level teaspoon is about 16 calories, so 2 teaspoons in 4 cups is 8 teaspoons, about 34 g.
  • Four cutting chais with a teaspoon of sugar each come to about 125, close to the 115 of 4 full cups without sugar.
  • Buffalo milk raises the day: 4 cups with 50 ml of buffalo milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar each are about 345.

How many calories are in one cup of chai?

A cup of home masala chai with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar has about 60 calories, about 30 without sugar, and a cutting chai with a teaspoon about 30. ICMR-NIN's Dietary Guidelines for Indians count a cup of tea with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar at 75 calories, close to the same. The recipe, the milk and sugar options and the arithmetic are on the masala chai calories page; everything below multiplies that cup by the day.

How many calories are in 2, 3 or 4 cups of chai a day?

Most people don't stop at one: two cups at home, one at office and one in the evening is four. With 2 teaspoons of sugar each, that's about 245 calories in the day from chai alone. With toned milk and 50 ml per cup:

A large mug can be twice a cup, so count it as two. Instant chai premixes and sachets already contain sugar and milk solids, so count them by their label rather than as home chai.

How much sugar is in your chai over a day?

A level teaspoon of sugar weighs about 4.2 g, according to USDA, and has about 16 calories. Two teaspoons in each of 4 cups is 8 level teaspoons, about 34 g of sugar: the difference between about 245 for the day and about 115 without any. In many homes the sugar spoon is heaped, which puts more into every cup than these numbers assume. If yours is, count each teaspoon as a little more than a level one.

Dropping one teaspoon from every cup takes 4 cups from about 245 to about 180.

Jaggery and sugar are close per gram: IFCT 2017 puts jaggery at 354 calories per 100 g, and USDA puts white sugar at 387. Switching from one to the other changes the flavour more than the day's number.

Does cutting chai change the day more than cutting the sugar?

A cutting chai at a tapri is half a glass, about half a cup, usually with a teaspoon of sugar. Four cutting chais come to about 125 calories for the day, close to the 115 of 4 full cups without sugar, and against about 245 for 4 full cups with 2 teaspoons each. Halving the cup and dropping the sugar land in much the same place.

What does the milk do over a day?

The milk sets the rest. Toned milk (3% fat) has about 58 calories per 100 ml, going by Amul Taaza's label. IFCT 2017 puts whole cow's milk at about 73 and buffalo milk at about 107. A cup with 50 ml of buffalo milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 85 calories, against about 60 with toned milk, so 4 cups of buffalo-milk chai come to about 345 for the day.

Milkier chai, made with half milk and half water or all milk, has more again. Each extra 100 ml of toned milk in a cup adds about 58 calories, so a day of doodh patti made almost entirely with milk can be several times a day of everyday chai. If you make chai with 100 ml of milk per cup, count the milk twice.

A tumbler of filter coffee with whole milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 105, more than chai mostly because it has more milk, so a day of coffee adds up faster.

What about tapri and station chai?

Chai from a tapri or a railway station is often sweeter and milkier than home chai, sometimes made with buffalo milk or condensed milk. We found no lab measurement of tapri chai, so as a range, assume up to a teaspoon more sugar and a little more full-fat milk: about 60 to 105 calories a cup. Over 3 or 4 cups the top of that range is well above the home figure, so count outside cups at the top of it unless you know the stall.

Biscuits, rusk and namkeen with chai are counted separately, and often add more than the chai itself.

What adds nothing over the day?

Tea leaves, a longer boil for a kadak cup, grated ginger, crushed cardamom and chai masala add almost nothing, however many cups you have. Lemon tea and black tea without sugar are close to zero. Green tea without sugar is the same.

How do you count chai without thinking about it?

Chai is easy to forget because it's small and frequent. The simplest habit is to decide what your usual cup is, with your milk and your sugar, and count each cup as that. Questions people ask

How many calories are in 4 cups of chai a day?

About 245 with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar in each cup, about 180 with 1 teaspoon, and about 115 without sugar.

How many calories are in 3 cups of chai a day?

About 185 with 2 teaspoons of sugar in each cup, about 135 with 1 teaspoon, and about 85 without sugar, for home chai with toned milk.

How many calories are in 5 cups of chai a day?

About 310 with 2 teaspoons of sugar in each cup, and about 145 without sugar.

How much sugar is in my chai over a day?

Count the teaspoons. Two level teaspoons in each of 4 cups is 8 teaspoons, about 34 g of sugar at about 16 calories each. A heaped teaspoon holds more.

Does cutting chai have fewer calories over a day?

Yes. Four cutting chais with a teaspoon of sugar each come to about 125, against about 245 for 4 full cups with 2 teaspoons each.
