How many calories are in your chai, and what changes them

By Nirmal Amin, Sonal Systems. Published 11 October 2026.

A cup of home chai made with toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar has about 60 calories, and about 30 without sugar. Sugar, the kind of milk and how many cups you drink in a day decide most of the number.

Key takeaways

  • Sugar is the biggest single variable: each level teaspoon is about 16 calories, and a heaped teaspoon holds more.
  • Milk comes next. Toned milk has about 58 calories per 100 ml, whole cow's milk about 73 and buffalo milk about 107.
  • Tea leaves, ginger, cardamom and chai masala add almost nothing.
  • Four cups a day with 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 240 calories.

What's in a cup

A typical home cup of chai is mostly water, boiled with tea leaves and spices, with some milk and sugar. ICMR-NIN's Dietary Guidelines for Indians count a cup of tea made with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar at 75 calories. Worked out from IFCT 2017 and USDA values, the same cup comes to about 60, depending on how full the teaspoons are.

Sugar: the biggest part

A level teaspoon of sugar weighs about 4.2 g, according to USDA, and has about 16 calories. In many homes the sugar spoon is heaped, which puts more into the cup than the numbers here assume. If your teaspoons are heaped, count each one as a little more than a level one.

Jaggery and sugar are close per gram: IFCT 2017 puts jaggery at 354 calories per 100 g, and USDA puts white sugar at 387. Switching from one to the other changes the flavour more than the number.

Milk: toned, whole or buffalo

The milk sets the rest. Toned milk (3% fat) has about 58 calories per 100 ml, going by Amul Taaza's label. IFCT 2017 puts whole cow's milk at about 73 and buffalo milk at about 107. A cup with 50 ml of buffalo milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 85 calories, against about 60 with toned milk.

Milkier chai, made with half milk and half water or all milk, has more again. If you make chai with 100 ml of milk per cup, count the milk twice.

Cup size and how many cups

A cutting chai at a tapri is half a glass, about half a cup. A large mug can be twice a cup. And most people don't stop at one: two cups at home, one at office and one in the evening is four. With 2 teaspoons of sugar each, that's about 240 calories in the day from chai alone.

Biscuits, rusk and namkeen with chai are counted separately, and often add more than the chai itself.

Milkier chai moves every number up. Each extra 100 ml of toned milk in a cup adds about 58 calories, so a cup of doodh patti made almost entirely with milk can be several times a cup of everyday chai. A tumbler of filter coffee with whole milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 105, more than chai mostly because it has more milk.

Instant chai premixes and sachets already contain sugar and milk solids, so count them by their label rather than as home chai.

Tapri and station chai

Chai from a tapri or a railway station is often sweeter and milkier than home chai, sometimes made with buffalo milk or condensed milk. We found no lab measurement of tapri chai, so as a range, assume up to a teaspoon more sugar and a little more full-fat milk: about 60 to 105 calories a cup.

What doesn't change it

Tea leaves, a longer boil for a kadak cup, grated ginger, crushed cardamom and chai masala add almost nothing. Lemon tea and black tea without sugar are close to zero. Green tea without sugar is the same.

Counting chai without thinking about it

Chai is easy to forget because it's small and frequent. The simplest habit is to decide what your usual cup is, with your milk and your sugar, and count each cup as that. Questions people ask

How many calories are in a cup of chai?

About 60 with toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar, and about 30 without sugar.

How many calories are in chai without sugar?

About 30 for a cup with 50 ml of toned milk. The milk is almost all of it.

How many calories does a teaspoon of sugar add?

About 16 for a level teaspoon (4.2 g). A heaped teaspoon holds more.

How many calories are in a cutting chai?

About 30 for half a cup with a teaspoon of sugar. Tapri cutting chai is often sweeter and milkier, so it can be more.

Does chai with jaggery have fewer calories than chai with sugar?

Very slightly. Per gram, IFCT 2017 puts jaggery at 354 calories per 100 g against 387 for sugar in USDA's data, so a teaspoon of either adds about the same.
