Longer pieces on counting calories in the food Indians actually eat: why your katori matters, where restaurant calories come from, what's in your chai, and what maintenance calories really are.

Every number here is worked out from a recipe in grams and Indian food composition data, and each article links its sources. The arithmetic for each dish is on its page in Calories in Indian food.
