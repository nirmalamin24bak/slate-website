Chaas calories

A 200 ml glass of home chaas (curd whisked with about three times as much water) has about 30 calories.

Chaas is curd whisked with water and salt, often with roasted cumin, ginger, green chilli, mint or coriander as masala chaas. It's drunk with lunch across Gujarat, Rajasthan and Punjab, and in the south as majjige or neer mor.

Most of a glass is water, so chaas is one of the lightest drinks there is. The amount of curd in it sets the number.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Restaurant and packed chaas is sometimes thicker than home chaas, with more curd per glass. A thali restaurant's chaas is often refilled freely.

What changes the number

  • The curd is the whole number. More water lowers it; a thick chaas can be twice as much.
  • Curd from toned or skimmed milk lowers it further.
  • Cumin, ginger, mint, coriander and salt add almost nothing.
  • Sweet lassi adds sugar: see lassi.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the salted lassi in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g curd whisked with half a cup of water and 4 to 5 ice cubes, with roasted cumin. Curd values are from USDA's whole-milk yogurt, since IFCT 2017 has no curd.

The thick chaas row adds curd so a 200 ml glass is half curd and half water.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 glass of chaas?

About 30 in a 200 ml glass, and about 60 for thick chaas made with equal curd and water.

How much protein is in chaas?

About 2 g in a 200 ml glass.

Is chaas the same as buttermilk?

In India, yes: chaas is diluted curd. Western cultured buttermilk is thicker and has more per glass.

Which has fewer calories, chaas or lassi?

Chaas, by a lot. A glass of sweet lassi has more curd and sugar, about 140.
