Egg curry calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

Egg curry has about 185 calories for each egg with its share of gravy, so a bowl with 2 eggs is about 365.

Egg curry is hard-boiled eggs simmered in an onion and tomato masala with ginger, garlic and ground coriander. Bengali dim kosha fries the eggs first, South Indian muttai kuzhambu adds tamarind or coconut, and dhaba anda curry is often oilier and spicier.

The egg itself is a little over a third of the calories here. The oil in the masala is more, so the gravy matters as much as the number of eggs.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home egg curry compare with outside?

Dhaba and restaurant egg curry usually comes with two eggs in a bowl, often fried before they go into the gravy, with more oil in the masala.

No lab has measured it, so the range below assumes 1.5 to 2 times the oil of this recipe for two eggs.

What changes the calories in egg curry?

  • Each egg adds about 70 calories on its own, with about 7 g of protein.
  • Oil is the other big part. Each teaspoon in the masala adds about 40 calories.
  • Frying the eggs first adds a little more oil; coconut milk in the gravy adds about 140 calories per 60 g.
  • Egg bhurji and omelettes are counted by their eggs and the oil in the pan.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the egg curry in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 1 egg with tomato, onion, ginger, garlic, green chilli and ground coriander, cooked in 2 teaspoons of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The egg is taken as 50 g without its shell, as the databank does. ICMR-NIN counts one boiled egg at 90 calories, which matches a larger egg of about 60 g.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in egg curry with 2 eggs?

About 365 for 2 eggs with their gravy, and about 550 for 3.

How much protein is in egg curry?

About 8 g for each egg with its gravy, so about 16 g for 2 eggs.

How many calories are in egg curry and rice?

Two eggs with gravy are about 365, and a 200 ml katori of plain rice adds about 160.

How many calories are in 100 g of egg curry?

About 120 for this recipe.
