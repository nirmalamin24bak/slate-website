Baingan bharta calories

A 200 ml katori of home baingan bharta has about 130 calories. A whole textbook bowl, made from 200 g of brinjal with a tablespoon of oil, is about 210.

Baingan bharta is a large brinjal roasted over a flame until the skin chars, then peeled, mashed and cooked with onion, tomato, green chilli and ginger. Punjabi bharta is cooked in oil or ghee, Bihari chokha is mixed with mustard oil raw, and Gujarati ringan no olo and Maharashtrian bharit are close cousins.

Brinjal is one of the lightest vegetables, about 25 calories per 100 g. Over half of this bowl's calories come from the oil.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the number

  • Oil or ghee is the biggest part. Each teaspoon adds about 40 to 45 calories to the pot.
  • Roasting drives water out of the brinjal, so a well-roasted bharta packs more into a katori than this recipe suggests.
  • Bihari chokha uses a spoon of raw mustard oil instead of frying; the calories per teaspoon are the same.
  • Peas in matar baingan bharta add a little, about 40 calories per 50 g.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the brinjal bharta in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 200 g brinjal, 50 g each of onion and tomato, green chilli, ginger and spices, cooked in a tablespoon of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The cooked weight is the ingredients added together, with no allowance for water lost in roasting, so the katori figures are on the low side for a dry bharta.

That works out to about 65 calories per 100 g of cooked baingan bharta. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

ICMR-NIN's general figure for a cup of dry vegetable dish is 150 calories, a little above this page's 130. A drier, well-roasted bharta, or one with more oil, comes closer to that, so a 200 ml katori is usually between 130 and 150.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of baingan bharta?

About 130 in a 200 ml katori, and up to about 150 for a drier bharta.

How many calories are in 100 g of baingan bharta?

About 65 for this recipe.

How much protein is in baingan bharta?

About 3 g in a 200 ml katori. Brinjal has little protein.

How many calories are in baingan bharta without oil?

Much less. Without the tablespoon of oil, the whole 324 g bowl is about 90 calories.
