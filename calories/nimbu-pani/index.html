Nimbu pani calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

A glass of nimbu pani has about 10 calories without sugar and about 40 with 2 teaspoons of sugar. Sugar is almost the whole number.

Nimbu pani is fresh lime or lemon juice in cold water with sugar, salt and often roasted cumin and black salt, as shikanji in north India. It's the summer drink at home, at roadside stalls and at railway stations.

Lemon juice and water have almost no calories, so each spoon of sugar decides the number.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home nimbu pani compare with outside?

Street shikanji and nimbu soda are usually sweet, often made with sugar syrup. Nimbu soda uses soda water, which has no calories.

No lab has measured one, so the range below allows 2 to 4 teaspoons of sugar a glass. ICMR-NIN's figure for a glass of fresh lime juice, 60 calories, falls inside it.

What changes the calories in nimbu pani?

  • Sugar is the whole number: each level teaspoon adds about 16 calories.
  • Honey adds about 21 calories a teaspoon, a little more than sugar.
  • Black salt, roasted cumin and mint add nothing measurable.
  • Ready-made lemonade and sweetened drinks can have much more sugar per glass.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the lemonade in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): the juice of 1 lemon in 1.5 cups of water with mint and crushed ice, sweetened with 3 tablespoons of sugar, for 2 glasses. This page counts the sugar per glass so you can see each amount. Lemon juice values are from IFCT 2017, sugar and honey from USDA.

One lemon is taken as 48 g of juice (USDA). A level teaspoon of sugar is 4.2 g and a level tablespoon 12.5 g (USDA).

Every dish on Slate is worked out the same way: how Slate counts calories.

Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

ICMR-NIN counts a glass of fresh lime juice at 60 calories, which is about 3 to 4 teaspoons of sugar. The textbook glass here, with 1½ tablespoons, is about 80. Depending on the sugar, a glass is anywhere from about 10 to 80.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in nimbu pani without sugar?

About 10 for a glass with the juice of half a lemon.

How many calories are in nimbu pani with sugar?

About 25 with 1 teaspoon of sugar, 40 with 2, and about 80 with 1½ tablespoons.

How many calories are in nimbu pani with honey?

About 30 with a teaspoon of honey.

How many calories are in shikanji?

About the same as sweet nimbu pani: 40 to 75 for a street glass with 2 to 4 teaspoons of sugar.
