Vada pav calories

One vada pav has about 335 calories: about 200 for the fried batata vada and the rest for the pav. With a teaspoon of butter it's about 370.

Vada pav is Mumbai's everyday street food: a spiced potato vada dipped in besan batter and deep-fried, tucked into a soft pav with dry garlic chutney, green chutney and a fried green chilli. It's eaten standing at stalls outside stations and offices all day.

The fried vada is a little over half of the calories and the pav most of the rest. Butter on the pav, or a second vada, adds to it.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Street vadas are fried in oil that's used all day, and stall sizes vary from small to large. Some stalls brush the pav with butter or add cheese.

A lab study found fried foods took up about a fifth more fat after 32 fryings in the same oil. The range below uses that, with a vada pav up to 1.3 times this size.

What changes the number

  • Butter on the pav adds about 35 calories a teaspoon, and a slice of cheese about 60 to 70.
  • Dry garlic chutney has coconut, peanuts or sesame in it and adds a little; green chutney adds almost nothing.
  • A bigger vada, or oil that's been used many times, means more fat in each vada.
  • Without the pav, the vada alone is about 200.

How these numbers were worked out

The vada follows the potato bonda in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g potato, 20 g peas, onion, ginger, green chilli and cashew, coated in a batter of 2 tablespoons of besan and 2 teaspoons of rice flour. The textbook makes 4 small bondas; a Mumbai vada is bigger, so this page makes 2. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The databank counts all the frying oil as eaten. ICMR-NIN counts a potato bonda at 100 calories, so this page sets the oil taken up to match that: about 10 g per vada. The pav is 43 g, the size in the databank's pav bhaji recipe, valued from USDA's soft dinner roll.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 vada pav?

About 335 for a vada pav made at home with a 43 g pav, and about 370 with a teaspoon of butter.

How many calories are in vada pav without pav?

About 200 for the batata vada on its own.

How many calories are in 2 vada pav?

About 665 for two.

How much protein is in vada pav?

About 8 g, from the pav, the besan and the peas.

Which has more calories, vada pav or samosa?

A vada pav, because of the pav. A home samosa is about 165, against about 335 for a vada pav.
