Mixed veg sabzi calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A 200 ml katori of home mixed veg sabzi with cauliflower, peas and potato has about 150 calories, about half of it from the oil. A bowl from 120 g of vegetables is about 180. Each teaspoon of oil adds about 40 calories. Restaurant mix veg, with more oil and sometimes cream, runs about 185 to 285.

Mixed veg sabzi at home is usually whatever's in season, most often cauliflower, peas and potato with tomato and onion, cooked fairly dry. On restaurant menus, mix veg, kadai veg and veg jalfrezi are richer versions with more oil and sometimes cream.

The vegetables here add only about half the calories. Peas and potato carry most of that share, and the oil the rest.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home mixed veg sabzi compare with outside?

Restaurant mix veg, kadai veg and veg jalfrezi are usually cooked in more oil or butter, and some are finished with cream or cashew paste.

No lab has measured an Indian restaurant mix veg, so the range below allows 1.5 to 2.5 times this recipe's oil, plus up to a tablespoon of cream per katori.

What changes the calories in mixed veg sabzi?

  • Oil is about half the number. Each teaspoon adds about 40 calories to the pot.
  • More potato, peas or corn raises it; more cauliflower, beans, capsicum or carrot lowers it.
  • Paneer cubes add about 65 calories per 25 g.
  • Cream or cashew gravy in restaurant versions adds the most.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the cauliflower, pea and potato bhujia in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 40 g each of cauliflower, peas and potato with tomato, onion, ginger, garlic and lemon, cooked in 10 g of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The cooked weight is the ingredients added together. Katoris use USDA's 185 g per cup for cooked mixed vegetables.
