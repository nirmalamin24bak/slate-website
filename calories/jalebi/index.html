Jalebi calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 15 October 2026.

A 30 g jalebi has roughly 95 calories, 100 g about 320, and 250 g by weight about 805. This is an estimate: we found no lab analysis of jalebi. Syrup is the biggest unknown: a jalebi left to soak longer takes up more sugar. From a halwai, 2 jalebis run about 195 alone to 290 with rabri.

Jalebi is a fermented maida batter piped in spirals into hot ghee or oil, fried crisp, then dipped in warm sugar syrup with saffron and cardamom. It's eaten hot for breakfast with poha in Indore, with rabri in north India, and at every festival in between.

Two things decide its number: the oil the spirals take up in frying and the syrup they soak up afterwards. Both vary from one halwai to the next, so treat the figures here as a guide.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the calories in jalebi?

  • Syrup is the biggest unknown. A jalebi that's left to soak longer takes up more sugar.
  • Oil taken up in frying is the next. Thin, crisp spirals take up more for their size than thick ones.
  • Rabri on top adds about 100 calories per 50 g; poha on the side is counted separately.
  • Imarti, made from urad dal batter, is similar per gram.

How were these numbers worked out?

No standard textbook in the Indian Nutrient Databank has jalebi, and we found no lab analysis of it, so this is an estimate built in two steps, each stated. The batter is 100 g maida with 25 g curd and water. Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd and sugar from USDA.

First, the fried spirals are taken to have the composition of USDA's measured puri (24.9% fat, 28% water), another fried wheat dough. Second, they're taken to soak up their own weight of syrup made as in The Art & Science of Cooking's gulab jamun (200 g sugar in 125 ml water, about 61.5% sugar). The 30 g piece is a typical size, also an assumption.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 jalebi?

Roughly 95 for a 30 g jalebi, an estimate. Thin, small jalebis have less; thick, syrupy ones more.

How many calories are in 100 g of jalebi?

About 320, and about 805 for a quarter kilo.

How much sugar is in a jalebi?

About 9 g of added sugar in a 30 g jalebi by this estimate, a little over 2 teaspoons.

How many calories are in jalebi with rabri?

About 195 for 2 jalebis on their own, and up to about 290 with 50 g of rabri.
