Gulab jamun calories

One home gulab jamun made with khoa (about 43 g, drained of syrup) has about 155 calories. A spoon of syrup poured over adds about 45.

Gulab jamun is a soft ball of khoa and a little maida, fried slowly in ghee or oil until dark brown, then soaked in warm sugar syrup flavoured with cardamom and rose water. It's the sweet at most Indian weddings and festivals, and kala jamun and Bengal's lal mohan are close cousins.

About half of a gulab jamun's weight is sugar it has soaked up. That sugar is close to half its calories, and the khoa and the frying fat most of the rest.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Sweet-shop and wedding gulab jamuns are often bigger than home ones, and they're usually served with more syrup. Brands analysed in a dairy science study had no more fat than this one, between about 6.5% and 12.3%.

What changes the number

  • Size matters most: a large sweet-shop gulab jamun can be half as big again.
  • Each tablespoon of syrup poured over adds about 45 calories, almost all sugar.
  • Gulab jamun made from a packet mix with milk powder is close per gram.
  • Served warm with a scoop of ice cream, add about 100 to 140 calories for the ice cream.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the gulab jamun in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g khoa, 25 g maida and a teaspoon of milk, making 6 gulab jamuns, fried and soaked in a syrup of 200 g sugar in 125 ml water. Values are from IFCT 2017, with sugar from USDA.

The databank counts all the frying fat and all the syrup as eaten, which is far too high. A lab analysis of khoa gulab jamun found 7.35% protein, 12.3% fat and 48.2% total sugar. This page uses the dough's protein to work out the finished weight, then sets the fat and sugar taken up to match: about 2 g of fat and 18 g of sugar per gulab jamun.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 gulab jamun?

About 155 for a home gulab jamun of about 43 g, drained, and about 200 with a tablespoon of syrup.

How many calories are in 2 gulab jamuns?

About 310 for two, drained.

How much sugar is in a gulab jamun?

About 18 g of added sugar in a 43 g gulab jamun, close to 4 teaspoons, plus the milk sugar in the khoa.

How many calories are in 100 g of gulab jamun?

About 355 for 100 g, drained of syrup.
