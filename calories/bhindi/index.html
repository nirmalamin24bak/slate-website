Bhindi sabzi calories

A 200 ml katori of home bhindi fry has about 155 calories. Okra is light; the oil is close to three quarters of the number.

Bhindi sabzi is okra cut into rounds and stir-fried with onion, turmeric, chilli and coriander until it loses its stickiness. Bhindi do pyaza adds more onion, Gujarati bhinda nu shaak is often cooked with a little besan or peanut, and kurkuri bhindi is deep-fried.

Okra has only about 28 calories per 100 g raw. Bhindi needs a fair amount of oil to cook dry, and in this textbook recipe that oil is nearly three quarters of the calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant bhindi masala and bhindi do pyaza are usually cooked with more oil, and kurkuri bhindi is deep-fried in batter, which takes up a lot more.

No lab has measured restaurant bhindi, so the range below allows 1.3 to 1.6 times this recipe's oil for the same katori.

What changes the number

  • Oil is almost the whole story. Each teaspoon adds about 40 calories, and okra soaks it up as it cooks.
  • Cooking in a wide pan on high heat needs less oil to stop the stickiness than covering the pan.
  • Besan or crushed peanuts in Gujarati bhindi add more: 10 g of peanuts is about 50 calories.
  • Kurkuri bhindi coated in besan and deep-fried has several times this oil.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the okra fry in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 120 g bhindi and 30 g onion with turmeric, coriander and chilli, cooked in 15 g of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The cooked weight is the ingredients added together. Katoris use USDA's 165 g per cup for cooked okra with fat added.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of bhindi sabzi?

About 155 in a 200 ml katori and about 115 in a 150 ml one.

How many calories are in 100 g of bhindi sabzi?

About 110, most of it from the oil.

How much protein is in bhindi sabzi?

About 3 g in a 200 ml katori. Okra has little protein; dal with it adds more.

How many calories are in 2 roti and bhindi sabzi?

Two medium rotis are about 190, and a 200 ml katori of bhindi about 155.
