Plain dosa calories

Checked on 14 October 2026.

One plain dosa (about 33 g of rice and urad dal, cooked with ¾ teaspoon of oil) has about 145 calories, or about 115 on a non-stick tawa with no oil.

A plain dosa is a fermented batter of rice and urad dal, spread thin on a hot tawa and cooked with a little oil, ghee or butter until crisp. It's eaten across south India for breakfast and dinner, with sambar and coconut chutney, and paper dosa is the same batter spread larger and thinner.

The batter is mostly rice. Without any fat a dosa is about 115 calories; the oil or ghee on the tawa is what moves it from there.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home plain dosa compare with outside?

Restaurant plain dosas and paper dosas are often much bigger than home ones, and many places finish them with butter or ghee. USDA's survey data lists dosas from 35 g for a small one to 155 g for a large one.

What changes the calories in plain dosa?

  • Fat on the tawa is the biggest variable. Each teaspoon of oil adds about 40 calories, and a teaspoon of ghee about 45.
  • A bigger or thicker dosa uses more batter. Paper dosa is thin but large, so it often has more than a home dosa.
  • Sambar and chutney are separate dishes. ICMR-NIN counts a cup of sambar at about 110 calories and 2 tablespoons of coconut chutney at about 120.
  • Rava dosa, neer dosa and pesarattu use different batters and have their own values. Masala dosa adds a potato filling: see masala dosa.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the plain dosa in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 50 g raw rice, 50 g parboiled rice, 30 g urad dal and a little fenugreek, making 4 dosas, cooked with 1 tablespoon of oil or butter between them. That's about ¾ teaspoon (3.4 g) per dosa. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Fermenting and cooking change the water in the batter, not the calories, so each dosa is counted as a quarter of the batter plus the fat on the tawa.

Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

Published figures for one dosa differ because dosas differ in size. ICMR-NIN counts a dosa at 125 calories, and USDA's survey data puts a medium 80 g dosa at about 170. A home plain dosa usually lands between 115 and 170, depending on size and oil.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 plain dosa?

About 145 for a home dosa cooked with ¾ teaspoon of oil, and about 115 without oil. A large restaurant dosa can be close to twice that.

How many calories are in a dosa with ghee?

About 160 with a teaspoon of ghee on the tawa.

How many calories are in dosa with sambar and chutney?

Add them separately. ICMR-NIN counts a cup of sambar at about 110 calories and 2 tablespoons of coconut chutney at about 120, so a dosa with both is about 375 to 400.

Which has fewer calories, dosa or idli?

Per piece, an idli at about 50 calories, because a dosa uses more batter and is cooked with oil. Two idlis are about 105, a little less than one dosa.

How much protein is in a plain dosa?

About 4 g, from the rice and urad dal. Sambar adds a few grams more.

How many calories are in dosa with sambar?

About 345 calories for one dosa and a 200 ml katori of sambar, with about 11 g of protein.
