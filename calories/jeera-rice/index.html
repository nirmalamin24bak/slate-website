Jeera rice calories

A 200 ml katori of jeera rice has about 185 calories, against about 160 for plain rice. A plate made from 80 g of raw rice is about 375.

Jeera rice is rice cooked with cumin seeds fried in oil or ghee, sometimes with a little onion. It's the standard rice on north Indian restaurant menus, served with dal fry, dal makhani and paneer dishes.

It's plain rice with a tempering, so the oil or ghee is the only real difference from steamed rice. In this recipe it's about a fifth of the calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the number

  • Oil or ghee is the only real difference from plain rice. Each teaspoon adds about 40 to 45 calories to the pot.
  • A heaped katori holds more than a level one.
  • Basmati and everyday rice are within a few percent of each other per gram.
  • Ghee rice and peas pulao add more fat or vegetables: see pulao.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the cumin pulao in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g rice with cumin seeds, 20 g onion and 2 teaspoons of oil, cooked in 160 ml of water. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Katoris use the plain rice page's 0.67 g per ml, from USDA's cooked white rice.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of jeera rice?

About 185 in a 200 ml katori and about 140 in a 150 ml one.

How many calories are in 100 g of jeera rice?

About 140.

How many calories are in jeera rice and dal fry?

A 200 ml katori of jeera rice is about 185, and a 200 ml katori of dal fry about 180.

How much protein is in jeera rice?

About 3 g in a 200 ml katori. Dal or curd with it adds more.
