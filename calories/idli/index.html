Idli calories

One medium idli (about 38 g) has about 50 calories, so 2 medium idlis come to about 105, before sambar and chutney.

Idli is a steamed cake of fermented rice and urad dal batter, usually three or four parts rice to one part dal. There's no oil in the batter, so an idli's calories come almost entirely from the rice and dal in it.

Sizes vary a lot, from small home idlis to the large ones in many restaurants. Kanchipuram idli is a different dish: it's made with ghee, sesame oil and pepper, so these numbers don't apply to it.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant idlis are often larger than home ones, and some places serve them with ghee or with podi mixed in oil. The batter is the same.

Without a lab figure for restaurant idli, the range below assumes idlis up to half as big again with up to a teaspoon of ghee. Sambar and chutney come on the side and are counted separately.

What changes the number

  • An idli's calories follow the rice and dal in it, so a large idli has about twice a small one.
  • A teaspoon of ghee, or of oil with podi, adds about 40 to 45 calories.
  • Sambar and coconut chutney are separate dishes. Coconut chutney is the richer of the two: ICMR-NIN counts 2 tablespoons at about 120 calories.
  • Rava idli is made with semolina and curd, often with a ghee tadka, and has its own values.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the idli in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g parboiled rice, 20 g urad dal and 120 ml water, making 8 small idlis. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Cooked weight is the batter weight, with nothing allowed for steaming, as the databank does. A medium idli here (about 38 g) is close to USDA's 38 g idli.

That works out to about 140 calories per 100 g of cooked idli. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

Published values for one idli run from about 35 calories for a small textbook idli to 75 for ICMR-NIN's idli. The difference is size, so pick the row that matches your idlis.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 idli?

A medium idli has about 50 calories. Small idlis are about 35 and large ones about 70.

How many calories are in 2 idlis with sambar?

2 medium idlis are about 105 calories. Sambar is extra: ICMR-NIN counts a cup of sambar at about 110 calories.

How much protein is in an idli?

A medium idli has about 2 g of protein, from the rice and the urad dal.

Does idli have any fat?

Very little: there's no oil in the batter, so a plain idli has well under 1 g of fat. Fat comes from ghee, podi oil and coconut chutney.
