Besan ladoo calories

One besan ladoo of about 35 g has about 165 calories. Ghee and sugar are about two thirds of it.

Besan ladoo is gram flour roasted slowly in ghee until it smells nutty, then mixed with powdered sugar and cardamom and shaped into balls. It's a Diwali and festival sweet in most of north and west India, and it keeps for weeks.

A ladoo is about a fifth ghee by weight, so it's rich for its size.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

A peer-reviewed study bought a besan ladoo from a Delhi vendor and tested it: 33 g with 7.9 g of fat, almost the same as this home ladoo. Sweet-shop ladoos are often bigger; USDA's survey data lists a ladoo at 60 g.

The range below runs from that measured ladoo to a 60 g one made like this recipe.

What changes the number

  • Size is the main thing: a 60 g sweet-shop ladoo has close to twice a 35 g one.
  • Ghee is about 40% of a ladoo's calories here. Each extra tablespoon in the batch adds about 110 calories.
  • Boondi ladoo and motichoor ladoo are fried besan pearls in syrup, and have their own values.
  • Nuts and raisins mixed in add a little.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the gram flour ladoo in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 50 g besan and 10 g suji roasted in 30 g ghee, mixed with 50 g powdered sugar and cardamom, making 4 ladoos. Values are from IFCT 2017, with sugar from USDA.

Besan is valued as ground Bengal gram dal, since IFCT 2017 has no separate besan entry.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 besan ladoo?

About 165 for a 35 g ladoo, and about 115 for a small 25 g one.

How many calories are in 100 g of besan ladoo?

About 470. USDA's survey value for a ladoo is 411.

How much sugar is in a besan ladoo?

About 12.5 g of added sugar in a 35 g ladoo, about 3 teaspoons.

How much protein is in a besan ladoo?

About 3 g, from the besan.
