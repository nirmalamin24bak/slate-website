Pani puri calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A plate of 6 pani puri with potato, chana and sweet chutney has about 220 calories, about 35 a puri. Ten come to about 370. The fried shells are most of it; sweet chutney adds about 20 calories a tablespoon. A street plate, 5 to 8 puris with more or less chutney, runs about 200 to 285.

Pani puri is a round, hollow, fried shell of semolina or wheat, cracked open and filled with potato, chana or ragda, sweet tamarind chutney and spiced mint-tamarind water, eaten in one bite. It's golgappa in Delhi, puchka in Kolkata and gupchup in Odisha.

The shells are fried, so they carry most of the calories. The pani itself is mostly water with a little tamarind; the potato and chutney add the rest.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home pani puri compare with outside?

Street pani puri varies with the stall: shell size, how much potato or ragda goes in, and how much sweet chutney you ask for. Most vendors give 5 to 8 to a plate and keep going if you want more.

We found no lab measurement of street pani puri, so the range below runs from 0.9 to 1.3 times this plate.

What changes the calories in pani puri?

  • The shells are most of it. Each puri with its share of the plate is about 35.
  • Sweet chutney adds about 20 calories a tablespoon; asking for extra adds up.
  • Ragda (white peas) in place of potato adds a little more protein and about the same calories.
  • The pani itself, mint, tamarind and spices in water, is about 15 calories for a whole plate.

How were these numbers worked out?

No standard recipe for this plate exists in the Indian Nutrient Databank, so the plate is assembled from typical amounts, each stated below and valued from IFCT 2017. Shells are a packaged pani puri's own label values (puris and boondi, 39 g for 5), since IFCT 2017 has no puri shells.

Sweet chutney as The Art & Science of Cooking's saunth chutney (ASC281: 25 g tamarind and 50 g jaggery in 100 ml water), so 1 tablespoon (15 g) holds 2.1 g tamarind and 4.3 g jaggery, about 21 kcal. The pani is 150 ml of water with 3 g tamarind and 10 g of mint and coriander.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 pani puri?

About 35 for one puri with its potato, chutney and pani.

How many calories are in a plate of pani puri?

About 220 for a plate of 6, and about 370 for 10.

How many calories are in pani puri water?

Very few. A plate's 150 ml of pani, with tamarind, mint and coriander, is about 15 calories. Sweet chutney adds more.

How much protein is in pani puri?

About 4 g in a plate of 6, from the shells and chana.

Which has more calories, pani puri or bhel puri?

A plate of bhel puri is about 265, a little more than a plate of 6 pani puri at about 220.
