Sev puri calories

A plate of 6 sev puri has about 295 calories, about 50 a piece. The fried papdi and sev are most of it.

Sev puri is flat, crisp papdi topped with boiled potato, onion and tomato, sweet and green chutneys and a heap of fine sev. Dahi sev puri adds curd, and papdi chaat in north India is the same idea with more curd and chutney.

The papdi and the sev are both fried, and together they're nearly three quarters of a plate's calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Street sev puri varies with how much sev and chutney the vendor piles on, and some add raw mango or butter.

We found no lab figure for it, so the range below runs from 0.9 to 1.3 times this plate.

What changes the number

  • Sev is the most calorie-dense topping: 15 g is about 80 calories.
  • Each papdi is about 20 calories before toppings.
  • Curd for dahi sev puri adds about 35 calories per 60 g.
  • Sweet chutney adds about 20 calories a tablespoon; green chutney adds almost nothing.

How these numbers were worked out

No standard recipe for this plate exists in the Indian Nutrient Databank, so the plate is assembled from typical amounts, each stated below and valued from IFCT 2017.

Papdi valued as maida plus oil at 38.5% fat, the fat content Gupta et al. 2016 measured in mathri (fried wheat crackers) from a Delhi vendor. 4 g per papdi is an assumption. Sev valued as besan plus oil at 39.6% fat, the fat content Gupta et al. 2016 measured in bhujia (fried besan noodles) from a Delhi vendor. Sweet chutney as The Art & Science of Cooking's saunth chutney (ASC281: 25 g tamarind and 50 g jaggery in 100 ml water), so 1 tablespoon (15 g) holds 2.1 g tamarind and 4.3 g jaggery, about 21 kcal.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 6 sev puri?

About 295 for a plate of 6, and about 335 for dahi sev puri.

How many calories are in 1 sev puri?

About 50 for one piece with its toppings.

How much protein is in sev puri?

About 5 g in a plate of 6, from the papdi, potato and sev.

Which has more calories, sev puri or pani puri?

Sev puri, per plate: about 295 against about 220 for 6 pani puri, because of the sev.
