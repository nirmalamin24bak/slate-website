Plain paratha calories

One plain paratha (about 27 g atta, cooked with 2 teaspoons of oil) has about 165 calories. Most of the difference from a roti is the oil.

A plain paratha is whole-wheat dough rolled out, brushed with oil or ghee, folded into a triangle or a square and rolled again, then cooked on the tawa with more fat. The folds make the layers. Lachha paratha is rolled into a coil for more layers and usually takes more ghee.

The atta is the same as in a roti, so the fat is what sets a paratha apart. In this textbook recipe, the oil is close to half of each paratha's calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Dhaba and restaurant parathas are often bigger than home ones and are cooked or finished with more ghee or butter. A lachha paratha has more layers and more fat again.

The range below runs from a paratha like this one up to one about 1.4 times the size with half as much fat again. The larger size matches USDA's 70 g paratha; the extra fat is our assumption.

What changes the number

  • Oil or ghee is the biggest variable. Each teaspoon used in the layers or on the tawa adds about 40 to 45 calories.
  • A teaspoon of butter on top adds about 35 calories, and a teaspoon of ghee about 45.
  • Parathas vary in size more than rotis. USDA's paratha weighs about 70 g cooked; this one about 49 g.
  • Kerala parotta is made with maida and more fat, and stuffed parathas carry their filling, so both have their own values. See aloo paratha.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the plain paratha in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of whole-wheat atta and 2 tablespoons of oil, making 3 parathas. All the oil is counted as eaten, since a paratha takes up what's on the tawa. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Cooked weight is worked out from water content: the atta's solids plus the oil, at the 33.5% water of USDA's cooked paratha.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 paratha?

A plain paratha made with about 27 g of atta and 2 teaspoons of oil has about 165 calories. ICMR-NIN counts one paratha at 150, close to this.

How many calories are in a paratha with ghee?

About 210 with a teaspoon of ghee on top, and about 200 with a teaspoon of butter.

Which has more calories, paratha or roti?

A paratha, because of the oil or ghee folded into it. A plain paratha is about 165 calories against about 95 for a medium roti, which has a little more atta.

How many calories are in a paratha without oil?

Without any fat it's a thick roti. The 27 g of atta in this paratha has about 85 calories on its own.

How much protein is in a paratha?

About 3 g in one plain paratha. The rest is mostly carbohydrate and fat: about 17 g and 9 g.
