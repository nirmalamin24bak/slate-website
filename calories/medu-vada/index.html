Medu vada calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

One medu vada from 15 g of urad dal, deep-fried, has about 70 calories. Two vadas, the usual plate, are about 140 before sambar and chutney, and about 340 with sambar. Size matters most: a vada from 20 g of dal has a third more. Restaurant or stall vadas, bigger and oilier, run about 140 to 225 for two.

Medu vada is a ring-shaped fritter of ground urad dal batter, deep-fried until crisp outside and soft inside. It's a breakfast staple in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala and Andhra, eaten with sambar and coconut chutney, or soaked in sambar as sambar vada.

Urad dal is high in protein for a fried snack. Oil taken up in frying is about a third of each vada's calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home medu vada compare with outside?

Vadas from an Udupi restaurant or a stall are often bigger than home ones and fried in oil that's been used many times, which raises the fat they take up.

A lab study found fried foods took up about a fifth more fat after 32 fryings in the same oil. The range below uses that, with vadas up to 1.5 times this size.

What changes the calories in medu vada?

  • Size is the main variable. A vada from 20 g of dal has a third more than one from 15 g.
  • Oil that's been used many times leaves more fat in each vada.
  • Sambar and chutney are separate. ICMR-NIN counts a cup of sambar at about 110 calories and 2 tablespoons of coconut chutney at about 120.
  • Sambar vada soaks two vadas in sambar: count the sambar too, see sambar.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the plain urad dal vada in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g of urad dal ground with a little water and deep-fried, making 4 vadas. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The databank counts all the frying oil as eaten, which is far too high. This page assumes a vada ends up with the composition of USDA's vada, 42% water and 9.7% fat, which works out to about 2.5 g of oil per vada and a vada of about 28 g. ICMR-NIN's 140 calories for 2 vadas agrees.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 medu vada?

About 70 for a home vada made from 15 g of urad dal.

How much protein is in medu vada?

About 3 g in one vada and about 7 g in two, from the urad dal.

Which has more calories, medu vada or idli?

A vada, a little: about 70 against about 50 for a medium idli. The vada is fried; the idli is steamed.

How many calories are in 2 medu vada with sambar?

About 340 calories: 2 vadas at 140 and a 200 ml katori of sambar. Two tablespoons of coconut chutney add about 120.
