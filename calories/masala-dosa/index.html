Masala dosa calories

A home masala dosa has about 255 calories with a teaspoon of oil on the tawa, and about 335 with a tablespoon.

Masala dosa is a thin, crisp crepe of fermented rice and urad dal batter, cooked with oil or ghee on a hot tawa and folded around a spiced potato and onion filling. Mysore masala dosa adds a red chutney inside; Udupi-style restaurants made it a breakfast staple across India.

Most of the difference between one masala dosa and the next is the oil or ghee on the tawa. The batter and filling vary much less.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant dosas are often larger, and ghee roast and butter masala dosas are cooked with a lot more fat on the tawa. Sambar and chutney come on the side.

No lab has measured a single restaurant dosa, so the range below assumes a dosa up to half as big again with 1 to 3 teaspoons of butter or ghee.

What changes the number

  • Each teaspoon of oil, ghee or butter on the tawa adds about 40 to 45 calories. Ghee roast and butter dosas can carry several.
  • A restaurant dosa can use half as much batter again as a home one.
  • More potato in the filling adds about 35 calories per 50 g; the onion and spices add little.
  • Sambar and chutney are separate dishes. ICMR-NIN counts a cup of sambar at about 110 calories and 2 tablespoons of coconut chutney at about 120.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the masala dosa in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g parboiled rice and 20 g urad dal for the batter, 100 g potato, 75 g onion and a teaspoon of oil for the filling, making 2 dosas. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The textbook fries each dosa with a tablespoon of oil. Many home cooks use far less, so the table shows a teaspoon, two teaspoons and a tablespoon.

ICMR-NIN counts a masala dosa at 200 calories. The textbook's dosa, with a tablespoon of oil, works out to about 335. Most of the gap is oil on the tawa, and some is size, so a masala dosa can reasonably be anywhere from about 200 to 335 at home.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 masala dosa?

A home masala dosa has about 255 calories with a teaspoon of oil on the tawa, and about 335 with a tablespoon. Restaurant dosas with butter or ghee run higher, about 300 to 515.

How many calories are in a plain dosa?

A plain dosa with a teaspoon of oil and no filling has about 180 calories.

Does masala dosa with sambar and chutney have more calories?

Yes. They're counted on their own: about 110 calories for a cup of sambar and about 120 for 2 tablespoons of coconut chutney, per ICMR-NIN.

How much protein is in a masala dosa?

A home masala dosa has about 6 g of protein, mostly from the urad dal and rice.
