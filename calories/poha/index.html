Poha calories

Checked on 14 October 2026.

A plate of poha made with 40 g of dry poha, with potato and peanuts, has about 315 calories.

Poha is flattened rice, rinsed and tossed in a little oil with mustard seeds, turmeric, curry leaves and green chilli. Maharashtra's kanda poha has onion; Gujarat's bateta poha has potato and usually a little sugar and lemon. Indori poha is steamed and topped with sev and jeeravan.

Plate sizes vary more than recipes, so this page counts poha by the dry poha that went in.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home poha compare with outside?

Poha from a stall or a station is usually a bigger plate, and it's often finished with sev, boondi or extra peanuts. In Indore it comes with jalebi on the side.

We found no lab measurement of stall poha, so the range below assumes a plate up to half as big again with a little more oil. Sev is fried, so a spoon of it on top adds more.

One plate made with 40 g dry poha. The outside range is 1 to 1.5 times the plate with up to a teaspoon more oil. Sev on top isn't counted.

What changes the calories in poha?

  • Dry poha is the main ingredient, so a plate made with twice the poha has close to twice the calories.
  • Each teaspoon of oil adds about 40 calories. This recipe uses 10 g for two bowls.
  • 10 g of peanuts adds about 50 calories.
  • Sev, boondi and namkeen are fried and add calories quickly, while coriander, lemon and pomegranate add almost none.
  • Batata poha has a little more than kanda poha because of the potato, but the difference is small.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the poha in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 40 g of rice flakes, 30 g potato, 10 g peanuts, 10 g oil and mustard seeds, for two bowls. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Portions are counted by dry poha, because no reliable cooked weight for poha was found. ICMR-NIN counts a 200 ml cup of poha at about 270 calories, which fits a plate made with a little more poha than the textbook's 40 g.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 plate of poha?

A plate made with 40 g of dry poha, with potato and peanuts, has about 315 calories. A large plate with 60 g of poha is about 470.

How much protein is in poha?

A plate made with 40 g of dry poha has about 6 g of protein, some of it from the peanuts.

Does Indori poha have more calories?

Usually, because of the sev on top. The poha itself is similar; the fried topping is what adds up.

Which has fewer calories, poha or idli?

Per plate, idli is usually lower because it's steamed: 2 medium idlis are about 105 calories, against about 315 for a plate of poha. Toppings and chutney change both.

How many calories are in poha and chai?

A plate of poha with a cup of masala chai with 2 teaspoons of sugar comes to about 375 calories.
