Calories in Indian food

Calories, protein, carbs and fat for everyday Indian food, in the portions you actually eat: a roti, a katori, a plate, a cup.

Every number is worked out from a recipe in grams and the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017). Each page shows the recipe, the arithmetic and the sources.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.
