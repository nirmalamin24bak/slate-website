Rajma calories

A 200 ml katori of home rajma curry has about 210 calories and about 8 g of protein.

Rajma is red kidney beans, soaked overnight, pressure-cooked and simmered in an onion and tomato masala. It's the everyday Punjabi and Jammu dish behind rajma chawal, and Jammu's Bhaderwah rajma has its own GI tag.

The beans carry the protein and most of the calories. The rest comes from oil in the masala, and from cream or a ghee tadka in dhaba versions.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Dhaba rajma often gets a second tadka of ghee, and restaurant rajma is sometimes finished with cream or malai, so the difference is the extra fat.

The range below adds about a teaspoon of ghee and up to a tablespoon of cream to a 200 ml katori, in line with published recipe proportions.

What changes the number

  • The masala here takes about a teaspoon and a half of oil per serving. Each extra teaspoon of oil or ghee adds about 40 to 45 calories.
  • A tablespoon of cream or malai adds about 30 calories.
  • A thick, reduced rajma holds more beans in each katori than a thinner one with more gravy.
  • Rajma chawal is two dishes, so count the rice separately. A 200 ml katori of plain rice is about 160 calories.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the rajma curry from The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 30 g of dry red rajma, 50 g tomato, 25 g onion, ginger, garlic and 1.5 teaspoons of oil for one bowl. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Cooked beans are taken at 2.62 times their dry weight, the ratio between USDA's raw and boiled kidney beans. Katoris are worked out at 1 g per ml.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of rajma?

A 200 ml katori of home rajma curry has about 210 calories, and a 150 ml katori about 160.

How much protein is in rajma?

A 200 ml katori has about 8 g of protein. Most of it comes from the beans.

How many calories are in rajma chawal?

Add the two: a 200 ml katori of rajma is about 210 calories and a 200 ml katori of plain rice about 160, so about 210 plus 160 for the pair.

Why is rajma soaked overnight?

Soaking softens the beans so they cook evenly. The beans take up water, which has no calories, so the total doesn't change.
