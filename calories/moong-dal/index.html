Moong dal calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

A bowl of yellow moong dal made from 30 g of dry dal has about 105 calories, or about 145 with the textbook's teaspoon of oil in the tadka.

Dhuli moong dal is split, skinned green gram. It cooks quickly to a soft, light dal and is the one most homes make for children, for someone unwell, or with rice as khichdi. It's tempered simply with jeera and a teaspoon of oil or ghee.

The dal itself is close to toor dal in calories. The tadka is what changes the number from one home to the next.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home moong dal compare with outside?

Restaurant yellow dal is often moong or a moong-toor mix, served thicker than home dal and finished with ghee or butter.

With no lab figure to go on, the range below assumes up to 1.5 times the dal per serving and up to one more teaspoon of ghee.

What changes the calories in moong dal?

  • The tadka is the main variable. A teaspoon of oil adds about 40 calories, and a teaspoon of ghee about 45.
  • Whole green moong (sabut moong) has the skin and a little more fibre, and is within a few percent per gram.
  • Palak or lauki added to moong dal adds very few calories and a lot of volume.
  • Moong dal cooked with rice is khichdi: see khichdi.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the washed moong dal in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 30 g of moong dal cooked in a cup of water with turmeric, chilli and jeera, tempered with a teaspoon of fat. The fat is counted per portion here, so you can see the dal with and without it. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Katoris are worked out at 1 g per ml for a thin dal, with their share of the textbook's teaspoon of oil.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 bowl of moong dal?

About 105 for a bowl made from 30 g of dry dal, or about 145 with a teaspoon of oil in the tadka.

How many calories are in 100 g of raw moong dal?

326 calories, with 23.9 g of protein, according to IFCT 2017.

How much protein is in a bowl of moong dal?

About 7 g in a bowl made from 30 g of dry dal.

Which has more calories, moong dal or chana dal?

Chana dal, a little, because it has more fat. Raw, IFCT 2017 puts chana dal at 329 and moong dal at 326 calories per 100 g. See chana dal.
