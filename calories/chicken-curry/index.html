Chicken curry calories

A bowl of home chicken curry with 125 g of chicken has about 315 calories. A 200 ml katori of curry and pieces is about 255.

Home chicken curry is chicken cooked in a masala of fried onion, tomato, ginger, garlic and whole spices, with a little curd. Every region has its own, from Punjabi tari wala chicken to Bengali kosha mangsho-style curries and Kerala versions with coconut.

Chicken is about two thirds of the calories in this recipe and the oil most of the rest. The share of chicken to gravy in your katori makes a big difference.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant and dhaba chicken curries are usually cooked with more oil, often with a layer of it on top, and some add cream or butter.

No lab has measured one, so the range below assumes 1.5 to 2 times the oil of this home recipe for the same katori.

What changes the number

  • The share of chicken matters most. A katori that's mostly pieces has more than one that's mostly gravy.
  • Each teaspoon of oil adds about 40 calories to the pot. Skimming oil off the top takes some away.
  • Chicken with skin, or thighs and legs, have more fat than skinless breast.
  • Coconut-based curries add more: 60 g of coconut milk is about 140 calories.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the chicken curry in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 250 g chicken with 100 g each of onion and tomato, ginger, garlic, curd and whole spices, cooked in a tablespoon of oil, for 2 bowls. Values are from IFCT 2017.

IFCT 2017's chicken breast has more fat than USDA's (9 g per 100 g against 3 g), so these figures are at the higher end. The textbook adds no water; katoris are worked out at 1 g per ml.

That works out to about 130 calories per 100 g of cooked chicken curry. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

Published figures differ with the oil and the share of chicken. ICMR-NIN counts ¾ cup of chicken curry at 240 calories, above this page's 195, while USDA's survey puts 100 g at 107, below this page's 130. A 200 ml katori of chicken curry can be anywhere from about 215 to 320.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 bowl of chicken curry?

About 315 for a home bowl with 125 g of chicken, and about 255 for a 200 ml katori.

How many calories are in 100 g of chicken curry?

About 130 for this home curry. Values from other sources run from about 107 to 160, depending on oil and how much is chicken.

How much protein is in chicken curry?

About 29 g in a bowl with 125 g of chicken, and about 24 g in a 200 ml katori.

How many calories are in chicken curry and rice?

Add about 160 for a 200 ml katori of plain rice to the curry: about 255 for a 200 ml katori.

Which has more calories, chicken curry or butter chicken?

Restaurant butter chicken, usually, because of the butter and cream. Made at home from textbook recipes the two are close: about 255 per 200 ml katori here and about 260 for butter chicken.
