Masala chai calories

A cup of masala chai made with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar has about 60 calories. Without sugar it's about 30.

Masala chai is tea leaves boiled with water, milk and sugar, with ginger, cardamom and other spices. In Gujarat it's usually sweet and milky; Mumbai's cutting chai is strong and served as half a glass.

Tea leaves and spices add almost nothing. The calories are in the milk and the sugar, and the sugar is usually the bigger share.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Chai from a tapri or a station is often sweeter and milkier than home chai, sometimes made with buffalo milk. A cutting chai is half a glass, so it's about half of a cup.

There's no lab measurement of tapri chai, so the range below is an assumption: up to a teaspoon more sugar and a little more full-fat milk.

What changes the number

  • Sugar. Each level teaspoon adds about 16 calories. A heaped home teaspoon holds more.
  • Milk. Toned milk (3% fat) is the lightest common choice. Full-cream and buffalo milk add more per cup.
  • Cup size. A cutting chai is half a cup; a large mug can be twice one.
  • How many cups. Three cups a day with 2 teaspoons of sugar each adds up to about 60 times three.

How these numbers were worked out

The cup follows ICMR-NIN's own tea: 2 teaspoons of sugar and 50 ml of toned milk. Toned milk is from Amul Taaza's label (3% fat, FSSAI's toned milk standard), buffalo milk from IFCT 2017 and sugar from USDA FoodData Central, at 4.2 g per level teaspoon.

Water, tea leaves and spices add almost nothing, so the cup is counted from its milk and sugar.

Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

ICMR-NIN counts this cup at 75 calories, against about 60 here. Most of the gap is the teaspoon: a level teaspoon of sugar is about 4.2 g, and many kitchen spoons hold more. A cup with 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 60 to 75 calories.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in a cup of chai with sugar?

A cup with 50 ml of toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar has about 60 calories, and about 45 with 1 teaspoon.

How many calories are in a cutting chai?

About 30 calories with a teaspoon of sugar, since it's about half a cup.

Does chai without sugar have calories?

A little, from the milk: about 30 calories a cup with 50 ml of toned milk.

Does buffalo milk chai have more calories?

Yes. With 50 ml of buffalo milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar, a cup comes to about 85, because buffalo milk has about twice the fat of toned milk.
