Paneer butter masala calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A 200 ml katori of home paneer butter masala has about 300 calories, and 100 g about 150. A textbook bowl with 50 g of paneer is about 230. Paneer carries most of it, and each extra tablespoon of butter adds about 100 calories. A restaurant serving, with more paneer, butter and cream, runs about 390 to 725.

Paneer butter masala, or paneer makhani, is paneer in a tomato gravy finished with butter and a little cream. Some cooks add onion, cashew and a pinch of sugar. Shahi paneer is its paler cousin, richer in cashew and cream and lighter on tomato.

Paneer has about 258 calories per 100 g, so it's most of the dish. The butter and cream decide how much higher it goes.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home paneer butter masala compare with outside?

A restaurant serving usually has more paneer than a home katori, and more butter and cream on top. A published chef's recipe serves 125 g of paneer per person, two and a half times the textbook bowl.

We found no lab measurement of restaurant paneer butter masala, so the range below is built from that serving size and a richer finish.

What changes the calories in paneer butter masala?

  • Paneer carries most of the calories, and low-fat paneer has noticeably fewer.
  • Each extra tablespoon of butter adds about 100 calories to the pot.
  • A tablespoon of cream adds about 30 calories, and 10 g of cashew about 60.
  • Restaurant servings are often two or three times a home katori.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is paneer in butter sauce from The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g paneer, 100 g tomato, 50 g onion, ginger, garlic, green chilli, 3 teaspoons of butter, 2 teaspoons of cream and 1.5 teaspoons of sugar, for 2 bowls. Paneer, vegetables and spices are from IFCT 2017; butter, cream and sugar, which IFCT doesn't list, are from USDA FoodData Central.

The cooked weight is the ingredients added together, as the databank does. Katoris are worked out at 1 g per ml.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of paneer butter masala?

A 200 ml katori of home paneer butter masala has about 300 calories. A restaurant serving is usually 390 to 725.

How much protein is in paneer butter masala?

A 200 ml katori has about 14 g of protein, nearly all from the paneer.

How much fat is in paneer butter masala?

A 200 ml katori of the home version has about 19 g of fat, from the paneer, butter and cream.

Which has more calories, paneer butter masala or shahi paneer?

Usually shahi paneer, because its gravy is built on cashew and cream rather than tomato. Both depend heavily on how much butter and cream go in.
