Momos calories

One steamed veg momo (about 25 g) has about 40 calories. A plate of 6 is about 245, before the chutney or mayonnaise.

Momos are dumplings of thin maida dough filled with cabbage, carrot, onion and ginger, or with minced chicken, then pleated and steamed. They came from Tibet and Nepal through Darjeeling and Sikkim, and are now sold at street carts across north India, steamed, fried or tandoori.

The maida wrapper is about four fifths of a veg momo's calories. Frying adds a lot; the red chutney adds little, and mayonnaise adds a lot.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Street carts sell momos steamed, fried, or tossed in sauce. Frying adds a lot: USDA's fried dumplings have about three times the fat of steamed ones. Mayonnaise on the side adds about 100 calories a tablespoon.

What changes the number

  • Fried or tandoori momos have much more fat than steamed ones.
  • Mayonnaise adds about 100 calories a tablespoon; the red chilli-garlic chutney adds very little.
  • Chicken momos are close per piece: USDA's steamed dumplings with meat are 113 calories per 100 g, against 125 without.
  • Paneer and cheese momos have more fat in the filling.

How these numbers were worked out

No standard textbook in the Indian Nutrient Databank has momos, so this uses a published dietitian's recipe from NephroPlus: 200 g maida with 100 g each of cabbage and carrot, 25 g spring onion, garlic, ginger, green chilli and pepper, cooked in 3 teaspoons of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The recipe's water isn't stated; 100 ml is assumed for a stiff dough and only affects weight. Each momo is taken as 25 g, the size USDA lists for a dumpling.

That works out to about 165 calories per 100 g of cooked momos. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

USDA's steamed dumpling with no meat is about 125 calories per 100 g, below this page's 165, because its wrapper is thinner. A plate of 6 steamed veg momos is usually between about 190 and 245.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 momo?

About 40 for a 25 g steamed veg momo.

How many calories are in a plate of momos?

About 245 for 6 steamed veg momos, 325 for 8 and 405 for 10, before chutney or mayonnaise.

How many calories are in fried momos?

About 345 for a plate of 6 fried, against 245 steamed.

How much protein is in momos?

About 6 g in a plate of 6 veg momos. Chicken momos have more.
