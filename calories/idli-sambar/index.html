Idli sambar calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

Two medium idlis with a 200 ml katori of sambar have about 305 calories and 10 g of protein. Three idlis with the same sambar are about 355, four about 410. The idlis are plain; the sambar's thickness and coconut set most of the difference between kitchens. Chutney isn't counted here. A restaurant plate runs about 225 to 400.

Idli sambar is the south Indian breakfast that travels everywhere: steamed rice and urad dal cakes with a katori of sambar poured over or alongside, and usually coconut chutney. In Tamil Nadu and Karnataka it's an everyday morning; in office canteens across India it's the light option.

This page adds up the site's own idli and sambar pages into one plate, so the number is exactly the sum of its parts. Coconut chutney has no page here yet, so it isn't counted: ICMR-NIN puts 2 tablespoons at about 120 calories, which is worth adding yourself.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home idli sambar compare with outside?

Restaurant idlis are often larger than home ones, sometimes with ghee or podi oil, while restaurant and canteen sambar is usually thinner than the textbook one and refilled freely.

No lab has measured a plate of idli sambar, so the range below is the two dish pages' outside ranges added together: the low end is 2 medium idlis with a thin sambar, the high end 2 large idlis with ghee and a thick sambar.

What changes the calories in idli sambar?

  • How many idlis. Each medium idli adds about 50 calories: 1 idli with sambar is about 255, 2 about 305, 3 about 355, 4 about 410.
  • The size of the idli. Two large idlis with the same sambar are about 340; restaurant idlis are often this size.
  • The sambar. A thin everyday sambar without coconut is close to half this one per katori (see sambar), which takes a plate of 2 idlis down to about 225.
  • Chutney and ghee. Coconut chutney isn't counted here; ICMR-NIN counts 2 tablespoons at about 120 calories. A teaspoon of ghee takes the plate to about 350.
  • Podi mixed with oil, or a fried medu vada on the side, adds more than the idlis do.

How were these numbers worked out?

The plate is 2 medium idlis and a 200 ml katori of sambar, each made exactly as on its own page: the idli from the textbook batter in The Art & Science of Cooking (parboiled rice and urad dal, 15 g of rice and dal in a medium idli) and the sambar from the same book's recipe with toor dal, vegetables, tamarind and coconut. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Nothing is re-estimated here. Each row adds the pages' unrounded values and rounds once, so the plate always equals its parts. The recipes, the arithmetic and the cross-checks are on the idli and sambar pages. Coconut chutney is left out because the site has no chutney recipe yet; the page says so rather than guessing.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in idli sambar?

About 305 calories for 2 medium idlis with a 200 ml katori of sambar, before chutney. One idli with the same sambar is about 255.

How many calories are in 2 idli sambar?

About 305 calories: 2 medium idlis at 105 and a 200 ml katori of sambar at 200. With a thin canteen sambar it's closer to 225.

How many calories are in 4 idli sambar?

About 410 calories for 4 medium idlis with a 200 ml katori of sambar. Three idlis with the same sambar are about 355.

How much protein is in idli sambar?

About 10 g in a plate of 2 idlis with sambar, from the urad dal in the idlis and the toor dal in the sambar. Four idlis with sambar have about 14 g.

Does idli sambar with chutney have more calories?

Yes. Coconut chutney isn't counted on this page; ICMR-NIN counts 2 tablespoons at about 120 calories, so a plate of 2 idlis with sambar and chutney would be closer to 305 plus that.

Which has fewer calories, idli sambar or dosa sambar?

Idli sambar: about 305 calories for 2 idlis with a katori of sambar against about 345 for a plain dosa with the same sambar, because the dosa is cooked in oil.
