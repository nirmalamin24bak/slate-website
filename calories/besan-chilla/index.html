Besan chilla calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

One besan chilla (about 17 g of besan, cooked with ½ teaspoon of oil) has about 80 calories, or about 60 on a non-stick tawa with no oil.

Besan chilla is a thin savoury pancake of gram flour batter with onion, tomato, green chilli, ginger, coriander and ajwain, cooked on a tawa. In Gujarat it's pudla, and many homes stuff it with grated paneer or add vegetables to the batter.

Besan is higher in protein than wheat flour, about 22 g per 100 g in IFCT 2017. The oil on the tawa is what changes the number most.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home besan chilla compare with outside?

Chillas from a cafe or a breakfast stall are often bigger and cooked with more oil or butter, and paneer or cheese fillings are common.

No lab has measured them, so the range below allows two chillas up to 1.3 times this size, with 1 to 2 more teaspoons of oil.

What changes the calories in besan chilla?

  • Oil on the tawa is the main variable: each half teaspoon adds about 20 calories.
  • Paneer filling adds about 50 calories per 20 g, and cheese more.
  • Vegetables in the batter add very little.
  • Moong dal chilla is close per gram of flour: IFCT 2017 puts moong dal at 326 calories per 100 g, against 329 for besan (as chana dal).

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the besan chilla in the Indian Nutrient Databank's open recipe set: 100 g gram flour with onion, tomato, ginger, coriander, green chilli and spices, for 6 chillas. The recipe itself uses only ¾ teaspoon of oil for all six, so this page counts the tawa oil per chilla instead. Values are from IFCT 2017.

IFCT 2017 has no separate besan entry, so besan is valued as ground Bengal gram dal.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 besan chilla?

About 80 with half a teaspoon of oil, and about 60 without oil.

How many calories are in 2 besan chillas?

About 160 for two, each cooked with half a teaspoon of oil.

How much protein is in a besan chilla?

About 4 g in one chilla, and about 8 g with 20 g of paneer.

Which has more calories, besan chilla or roti?

They're close. One chilla with half a teaspoon of oil is about 80 calories, and a medium roti about 95. The chilla has more protein.
