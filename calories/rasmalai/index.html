Rasmalai calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 15 October 2026.

One rasmalai with its milk, about 77 g, has about 130 calories, and 2 about 260. The chhena patty is about half of that and the sweetened milk the rest, so the spoonfuls of ras served with it matter as much as the piece. Packed and sweet-shop rasmalai runs about 120 to 180 a piece, by their labels.

Rasmalai is a flattened rasgulla: chhena from cow's milk, kneaded, cooked in sugar syrup, then soaked in milk that has been simmered down with sugar, saffron and cardamom. It is served cold with a few spoonfuls of the milk, often with pista on top.

Unlike a rasgulla, the milk brings fat and milk sugar as well as added sugar, so a rasmalai with its ras is close to twice a rasgulla of the same patty.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home rasmalai compare with outside?

Packed rasmalai labels run from 190 calories per 100 g (Haldiram's Mitha Magic, 63 g servings) to 225 (Nanak, 80 g pieces), against this page's 170. A thicker rabdi-style ras, or buffalo milk, pushes a sweet shop's higher.

What changes the calories in rasmalai?

  • The milk served with it is half the number. Each extra tablespoon of this ras adds about 20 calories; a thick rabdi adds more.
  • Buffalo milk or full-cream milk in the ras adds fat; toned milk takes it off.
  • Sugar in the ras is about a teaspoon a piece here. Sweet shops often use more.
  • A rasgulla from the same chhena, in syrup instead of milk, is about 70.
  • Pista and saffron on top add nothing you can measure.

How were these numbers worked out?

The chhena patties follow the rasmalai in Basic Food Preparation (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): chhena from 250 ml of cow's milk, bound with half a tablespoon of maida and cooked in sugar syrup, making 2. IFCT 2017 has no chhena, so paneer stands in, with the chhena's weight worked out from the milk's protein at about 80% casein, an assumption; sugar is USDA's. A Hisar survey of market rasmalai, about 10.5% protein and 18% carbohydrate, sets the patty weight and the sugar taken up. The ras follows a published halwai recipe, about 55 ml of milk and a teaspoon of sugar per piece, simmered down by a third. One rasmalai with its milk is half the recipe, about 77 g and 130 calories; piece and milk together are 170 per 100 g. Haldiram's label at 190 calories per 100 g is 11% above, within 15%; it and Nanak's label set the shop range. Every dish on Slate is worked out the same way: how Slate counts calories.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 rasmalai?

About 130 for one piece of about 33 g with about 3 tablespoons of its milk, 77 g in all.

How many calories are in 2 rasmalai?

About 260 for two pieces with their milk.

How many calories are in rasmalai without the milk?

About 70 for the chhena patty alone, worked out from this recipe: a 33 g piece lifted out of the ras. The milk it sits in is the other 60 or so.

Which has more calories, rasmalai or rasgulla?

Rasmalai, about 130 with its milk against about 70 for a rasgulla drained of syrup. The sweetened milk carries fat and milk sugar that syrup does not.

How much sugar is in rasmalai?

About 9 g of added sugar in one piece with its milk: about 3.6 g soaked into the patty and a teaspoon in the ras, plus the milk's own sugar.
