Uttapam calories

One onion tomato uttapam (about 40 g of rice and urad dal, cooked with 1½ teaspoons of oil) has about 215 calories.

Uttapam is made from the same kind of rice and urad dal batter as dosa, but it's poured thick, topped with onion, tomato, chilli and coriander, and cooked slowly on both sides with oil. It's a breakfast and tiffin dish across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, and a standard on Udupi restaurant menus.

Because it's thick, one uttapam holds more batter than a home dosa. The toppings add little; the batter and the oil on the tawa make up most of the number.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant uttapams are often thicker and wider than home ones, and some places finish them with butter or serve a ghee version. The toppings are similar.

What changes the number

  • Thickness and size matter most. A thick uttapam can hold twice the batter of a home dosa.
  • Each teaspoon of oil on the tawa adds about 40 calories. Uttapam is cooked on both sides, so it usually takes more oil than a dosa.
  • The onion and tomato topping adds under 20 calories an uttapam.
  • Cheese or paneer uttapam adds more: a 20 g grating of cheese is about 60 calories.
  • Rava uttapam uses semolina and curd instead of rice and dal, and has its own values.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the onion tomato uttapam in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g rice, 20 g urad dal, 50 g each of onion and tomato and green chilli, cooked with 3 teaspoons of oil, making 2 uttapams. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The databank also lists a cup of oil 'for frying', which would make each uttapam over 600 calories. Uttapam is cooked on a tawa, so only the 3 teaspoons are counted here.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 uttapam?

About 215 for an onion tomato uttapam made at home with 1½ teaspoons of oil. A mini uttapam is about 55.

Which has more calories, uttapam or dosa?

Usually the uttapam, because it's thicker and holds more batter and oil. A home plain dosa is about 145 calories against about 215 for an uttapam.

How much protein is in an uttapam?

About 5 g, from the rice and urad dal.

Is uttapam batter the same as dosa batter?

Very close. Many homes use leftover dosa batter for uttapam, often after it's turned a little sour. The calories per gram of batter are about the same.
