Rasgulla calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

One rasgulla (about 36 g, lightly squeezed of syrup) has about 70 calories. With a tablespoon of syrup it's about 115.

Rasgulla is a spongy ball of chhena, fresh cheese made by curdling milk, kneaded smooth and cooked in light sugar syrup until it swells. It comes from Bengal and Odisha, where it's rosogolla and rasagola, and it's sold in tins and clay pots across India.

It isn't fried, so it has much less fat than gulab jamun. Most of its calories are the sugar it soaks up, and a rasgulla eaten straight from the syrup carries more.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home rasgulla compare with outside?

Tinned and sweet-shop rasgullas are often sweeter than home ones. The label on Aavin's rasgulla, from Tamil Nadu's milk co-operative, lists 273 calories for 100 g, more than this page's 200, mostly from sugar.

The range below runs from this rasgulla up to 1.4 times it, in line with that label.

What changes the calories in rasgulla?

  • Syrup is the main variable. Squeezing a rasgulla before eating takes off much of the sugar; a tablespoon of syrup adds about 45 calories.
  • Rasgullas made from full-fat milk or buffalo milk have more fat than those from toned milk.
  • Rasmalai soaks the chhena in thickened sweet milk instead of syrup, and has more fat and calories.
  • Low-sugar rasgullas made with sweeteners have far fewer calories.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the rasgulla in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): chhena from 265 ml of cow's milk, kneaded with half a tablespoon of maida and cooked in a syrup of 150 g sugar. IFCT 2017 has no chhena, so it's valued as paneer, the closest match. The chhena's weight is worked out from the milk's protein, taking about 80% of milk protein as the casein that stays in the chhena (an assumption).

A lab analysis of rasgulla found 4.8% protein and 35% total sugar. This page uses the chhena's protein to work out the finished weight, then sets the sugar taken up to match, about 11 g per rasgulla.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 rasgulla?

About 70 for a home rasgulla of about 36 g, squeezed, and about 115 with a tablespoon of syrup.

How many calories are in 2 rasgullas?

About 140 for two, squeezed of syrup.

Which has more calories, rasgulla or gulab jamun?

A gulab jamun, about 155 against about 70 for a rasgulla. The gulab jamun is fried and soaks up more sugar.

How much protein is in a rasgulla?

About 2 g, from the chhena.
