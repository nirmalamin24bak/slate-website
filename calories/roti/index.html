Roti calories

One medium roti (about 30 g atta) has about 95 calories, or about 140 with a teaspoon of ghee.

Roti, chapati and phulka are the same flatbread: whole-wheat atta and water, rolled out and cooked on a hot tawa. A phulka is held over an open flame at the end so it puffs. In Gujarat it's rotli, often smaller and thinner than a Punjabi roti.

A plain roti is almost all atta, so its calories follow the size of the dough ball. Ghee or butter on top is what usually raises the number.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

At home, most rotis reach the plate dry or with a little ghee. At a dhaba or in a restaurant they're often brushed with ghee or butter, and tandoori rotis are usually bigger, which adds more again.

No lab has published a figure for a restaurant roti, so the range below assumes a medium roti with 1 to 2 teaspoons of ghee.

What changes the number

  • Calories follow the atta, so a roti rolled from a bigger dough ball counts for more, however thin it's rolled.
  • A teaspoon of ghee or butter on top (5 g) adds about 45 calories, close to half a roti again.
  • Some kitchens knead the atta with a spoon of oil, which spreads about 45 calories per teaspoon across the whole batch.
  • These numbers are for whole-wheat atta. Rotis made with maida, or mixed with besan, bajra or jowar, have their own values.
  • A phulka loses a little water over the flame, so it weighs less, but its calories stay with the atta.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is 100 g of whole-wheat atta with about 70 g of water. Each roti's calories are its share of the atta, using IFCT 2017 values for atta and ghee.

Cooked weight comes from water content: IFCT's atta is about 11% water and USDA's cooked chapati about 33%, so a roti weighs about 1.33 times its atta.

As a check, ICMR-NIN lists one phulka at 80 calories, and 24 g of atta works out here to about 75. The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook, makes four chapatis from 80 g of atta, so each of its chapatis has only 20 g of atta.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 roti?

A medium roti made with about 30 g of atta has about 95 calories. A phulka is about 75, and a large roti about 130.

How many calories does ghee add to a roti?

One teaspoon of ghee (5 g) adds about 45 calories. A medium roti with a teaspoon of ghee comes to about 140.

Is a phulka different from a chapati?

They're made from the same dough. A phulka is puffed over an open flame and is usually smaller, so one phulka counts for less, but gram for gram of atta the calories are the same.

How many grams is one roti?

A medium roti made with 30 g of atta weighs about 40 g once cooked. Sizes vary a lot between kitchens, which is why Slate asks you for your roti size.

Which has fewer calories, roti or rice?

Per usual serving they're close: 2 medium rotis are about 190 calories, and a 200 ml katori of plain rice is about 160. Per 100 g, roti has more, because it holds less water: about 240 against about 120 for cooked rice.
