Aloo paratha calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

One home aloo paratha (27 g atta, 27 g potato, 2 teaspoons of oil) has about 190 calories, or about 225 with a teaspoon of butter. Two aloo parathas with curd come to about 440. Tawa oil matters most, about 40 calories a teaspoon. A dhaba paratha, larger and served with butter, runs about 225 to 390.

Aloo paratha is whole-wheat dough stuffed with spiced mashed potato, rolled out and cooked on the tawa with oil or ghee. It's a Punjabi breakfast staple, usually eaten with curd, pickle and a cube of white butter, and it's common in tiffins across north India.

The potato adds less than people expect: in this recipe it's about a tenth of the calories. The atta and the oil on the tawa make up nearly all the rest.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home aloo paratha compare with outside?

At a dhaba, an aloo paratha is often larger, cooked in more ghee or oil and served with a cube of butter melting on top. Amritsari and Murthal-style parathas are known for it.

No lab figure exists for a dhaba aloo paratha, so the range below assumes one up to 1.4 times this size, with up to half as much oil again and 1 to 2 teaspoons of butter.

What changes the calories in aloo paratha?

  • The oil on the tawa is the biggest variable. Each teaspoon adds about 40 calories to a paratha.
  • A teaspoon of butter on top adds about 35 calories, and a tablespoon about 100.
  • A thicker filling adds less than you'd think: 50 g more potato is about 35 calories.
  • A katori of curd on the side (100 g) adds about 60 calories, and pickle in oil adds more.
  • Gobi, mooli and methi parathas use lighter fillings than potato. Paneer parathas have more.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the potato paratha in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of atta, 80 g of potato, 25 g of onion, green chilli and coriander, with 2 tablespoons of oil for 3 parathas. Spices under 1 g each are left out; together they add under 3 calories a paratha. Values are from IFCT 2017.

All the oil is counted as eaten. Curd values are from USDA, because IFCT 2017 has no curd.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 aloo paratha?

A homemade aloo paratha made with about 27 g of atta and 2 teaspoons of oil has about 190 calories. Two have about 380.

How many calories are in aloo paratha with butter?

About 225 with a teaspoon of butter, and about 290 with a tablespoon. At a dhaba it can be more, because the paratha is often bigger too.

How much protein is in an aloo paratha?

About 3 g. With a 100 g katori of curd it's about 7 g.

How many calories are in aloo paratha with curd?

One aloo paratha with 100 g of curd comes to about 250 calories.

Which has more calories, aloo paratha or plain paratha?

The aloo paratha, by a little. Made from the same atta and oil, it's about 190 calories against about 165 for a plain paratha, because of the potato and onion.

How many calories are in 2 aloo parathas with curd?

About 440 calories: 2 parathas at 380 plus a 100 g katori of curd. With a teaspoon of butter on each instead of the curd, about 450.

How many calories are in 3 aloo parathas?

About 565 calories, with about 10 g of protein. One paratha with curd and a teaspoon of butter together is about 285.
