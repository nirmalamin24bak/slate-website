Puri calories

One home puri (about 13 g atta, deep-fried) has about 95 calories. Four puris have about 385, and a large 36 g puri about 140.

Puri is a small round of whole-wheat dough, deep-fried until it puffs. It's eaten with aloo sabzi as puri bhaji, with chole, or with shrikhand and aamras on festival days. Bengali luchi is the same idea made with maida.

Atta is about half of a puri's calories. Most of the other half is oil the dough takes up in the kadhai, so this page counts that oil from lab data.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Puris from a halwai or a puri-bhaji stall are often larger than home ones, and they're fried in oil that's been used many times, which raises the fat a puri takes up.

A lab study of Indian fried foods, puri among them, found total fat rose by about a fifth between the first and the 32nd frying in the same oil. The range below uses that, and a puri up to the 36 g of USDA's puri.

What changes the number

  • Size matters most. A puri rolled from 20 g of atta has half as much again as one from 13 g.
  • Oil that's been used many times leaves more fat in each puri: about a fifth more after 32 fryings, in one lab study.
  • Ghee or oil kneaded into the dough adds about 45 calories per teaspoon across the batch.
  • Masala puri, palak puri and Bengali luchi are close per gram. Kachori and bhatura have their own values: see bhatura.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the poori in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of whole-wheat atta with a teaspoon of melted fat, making 5 to 6 puris. This page uses 6. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Frying oil is harder to count. The databank counts all of it as eaten, which gives far too high a number. This page assumes a puri ends up with the fat content of USDA's puri, 24.9%, which works out to about 5 g of oil taken up per puri.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 puri?

A home puri made with about 13 g of atta has about 95 calories once fried. A large one, about 36 g, has about 140.

How many calories are in 4 puris?

About 385 for four home puris, before the sabzi. Three are about 285.

Which has more calories, puri or roti?

A puri has more for its size, because it takes up oil in frying. One small puri is about 95 calories; a medium roti with more than twice the atta is about 95.

How much oil does a puri absorb?

About 5 g, a little over a teaspoon, for a home puri fried in fresh oil, going by USDA's measured puri at about a quarter fat. Older oil and lower heat leave more.

Puri or bhatura, which has more calories?

A bhatura is bigger and made with maida, so one bhatura is about 185 calories against about 95 for a puri.
