Sweet lassi calories

A glass of home sweet lassi (100 g curd, about 4 teaspoons of sugar and water) has about 140 calories. A 200 ml glass is about 115.

Sweet lassi is curd churned with sugar and a little water or ice until frothy, sometimes with cardamom or rose water. In Punjab and at dhabas it's thick, served in tall steel glasses with a layer of malai on top.

Sugar is a little over half its calories in this recipe, and the curd the rest. Malai on top adds a lot.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the number

  • Sugar is the biggest part: each teaspoon adds about 16 calories.
  • Malai or cream on top adds about 30 calories a tablespoon.
  • Thick lassi with more curd per glass has more of everything.
  • Mango lassi adds mango pulp; salted lassi has no sugar, see chaas.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the sweet lassi in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g curd with 20 g sugar and half a cup of water, with ice. The ice adds water, not calories, so it's left out of the volume. Curd values are from USDA's whole-milk yogurt, since IFCT 2017 has no curd; sugar from USDA.

ICMR-NIN counts a 200 ml glass of lassi with 2 teaspoons of sugar at 110 calories, close to this page's 200 ml.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 glass of sweet lassi?

About 140 for a home glass with 100 g of curd and 20 g of sugar, and about 170 with a tablespoon of malai.

How many calories are in a dhaba lassi?

Roughly 210 to 405 for a big glass of thick lassi with malai.

How much sugar is in sweet lassi?

About 20 g of added sugar in this glass, close to 5 teaspoons.

How much protein is in lassi?

About 3 g in a glass, from the curd.
