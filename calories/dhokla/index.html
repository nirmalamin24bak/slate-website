Dhokla calories

One piece of besan dhokla (about 40 g) has about 80 calories. A plate of 4 pieces is about 315.

Dhokla, as most of India knows it, is the soft, yellow besan cake from Gujarat: gram flour and curd batter, leavened with soda and steamed, then tempered with mustard seeds, green chillies and curry leaves. In Gujarat the besan one is called khaman, and dhokla usually means a fermented rice and dal version.

It's steamed, so most of its calories come from the besan. Farsan shops pour a sweetened oil tempering over it, which adds more.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Khaman from a farsan shop is usually soaked in a tempering of oil, sugar and water, which makes it moist and slightly sweet. It's often served with a sweet chutney too.

What changes the number

  • The tempering matters most. Each teaspoon of oil poured over adds about 40 calories, and each teaspoon of sugar about 16.
  • Bigger pieces count for more: this page's piece is about 40 g.
  • Sweet tamarind or date chutney adds about 60 calories a tablespoon; green chutney adds very little.
  • Fermented rice and urad dhokla (white dhokla) and khandvi have their own values.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the dhokla in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 150 g besan and 150 g curd with ginger, green chilli and soda, steamed and tempered with a teaspoon of oil and mustard seeds, cut into 8 pieces. Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd from USDA.

Steaming keeps the water in, so the cooked weight is the ingredients added together: about 40 g a piece. Besan is valued as ground Bengal gram dal.

That works out to about 200 calories per 100 g of cooked dhokla. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

Basic Food Preparation's khaman, made from soaked chana dal, comes to about 65 calories a piece, below this page's 80, because its pieces are smaller. Depending on size and tempering, a piece of dhokla is usually 65 to 100 calories.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 piece of dhokla?

About 80 for a 40 g piece of besan dhokla, and more with a sweet tempering from a farsan shop.

How many calories are in 100 g of dhokla?

About 200, and about 500 for a 250 g box before the shop's tempering.

How much protein is in dhokla?

About 5 g in one piece and about 19 g in four, from the besan and curd.

Which has more calories, dhokla or idli?

Dhokla, per piece: about 80 against about 50 for a medium idli. Dhokla also has more protein.
