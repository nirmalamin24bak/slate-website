Kaju katli calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

One sweet-shop piece of kaju katli, about 10 g, has about 50 calories; a home piece of about 17 g is about 90. Four shop pieces, 40 g, come to about 200. Piece size is the main variable; the recipe, cashews and sugar, barely varies. Packed kaju katli, by a brand's label, runs about 180 to 200 for 4 pieces.

Kaju katli is ground cashews cooked with sugar syrup into a smooth dough, rolled thin, cut into diamonds and often topped with silver leaf. It's the most gifted sweet at Diwali and keeps for days without refrigeration.

Cashews and sugar are roughly equal by weight, but cashews carry more of the calories because of their fat. Per gram, kaju katli is one of the richest common sweets.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home kaju katli compare with outside?

Shop and packed kaju katli is made to much the same recipe. A packed brand's label lists 450 calories per 100 g, a little below this page's 505, which suggests a little less cashew or more sugar.

What changes the calories in kaju katli?

  • Piece size is the main thing: a shop piece is about 10 g, a home piece often more.
  • More cashew and less sugar raises it slightly per gram, since cashews have more calories than sugar.
  • Silver leaf adds nothing measurable.
  • Kaju roll, kaju pista roll and kaju barfi with khoa are close per gram.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the cashewnut burfi in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 50 g cashews ground and cooked with 50 g sugar in 3 tablespoons of water, making 6 pieces. Values are from IFCT 2017, with sugar from USDA.

The databank counts all 2 tablespoons of ghee used for greasing the tray as eaten. This page counts 5 g of it.
