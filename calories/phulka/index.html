Phulka calories

One phulka (about 24 g atta, no oil) has about 75 calories. Two phulkas have about 155, and a phulka with half a teaspoon of ghee about 100.

A phulka is a thin whole-wheat roti, cooked briefly on the tawa and then held over an open flame until it puffs. There's no oil in the dough or on the tawa. It's the everyday bread in many Gujarati homes, where it's called rotli, and in Telugu homes, where it's pulka.

Because phulkas are small, most people count them by how many are on the plate. Each one is almost all atta, so the count and any ghee spread on top decide the total.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Phulkas from a thali restaurant or a tiffin service are usually about the size of home ones, but they often arrive brushed with ghee, even when you didn't ask for it.

What changes the number

  • The atta in each phulka sets its number. A small one made from 20 g of atta has about 65 calories; one made from 30 g, the size of a medium roti, about 95.
  • Half a teaspoon of ghee adds about 20 calories, and a full teaspoon about 45.
  • The dry atta used for dusting sticks to the phulka and adds a little, far less than ghee.
  • Puffing over the flame drives off water. The calories stay the same.
  • Bajra, jowar or multigrain atta shifts the number a little. Per 100 g, IFCT 2017 puts bajra at 348 calories and jowar at 334, against 320 for wheat atta.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the chapati in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of whole-wheat atta kneaded with 4 tablespoons of water. The textbook makes four chapatis from it, the small phulka in the table. Its teaspoon of ghee is counted separately here, per phulka, because many homes skip it.

Values for atta and ghee are from IFCT 2017. Cooked weight comes from water content: IFCT's atta is about 11% water and USDA's cooked chapati about 33%, so a phulka weighs about 1.33 times its atta.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 2 phulkas?

Two phulkas made with about 24 g of atta each have about 155 calories. Three have about 230, and four about 305.

How many calories are in a phulka with ghee?

About 100 with half a teaspoon of ghee, and about 120 with a full teaspoon. A plain phulka is about 75.

Are phulka and roti the same?

They're the same dough. A phulka is usually smaller, is puffed over an open flame and is made without oil, so one phulka counts for less than a medium roti at about 95 calories.

How much protein is in a phulka?

About 3 g in a phulka made from 24 g of atta, so 3 phulkas give about 8 g. Dal or curd on the side adds more.

How many calories are in 100 g of phulka?

About 240 calories per 100 g of cooked phulka. That's more than rice per 100 g, because a phulka holds much less water.
