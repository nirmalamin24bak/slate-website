Bhel puri calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A plate of bhel puri, 25 g of puffed rice with sev, papdi, potato, onion, peanuts and chutneys, has about 265 calories. Half a plate is about 130, and 100 g about 235. Sev is the most calorie-dense part, about 55 calories per 10 g. A street plate, mixed to order, runs about 240 to 370.

Bhel puri is puffed rice tossed with fine sev, crushed papdi, boiled potato, onion, tomato and peanuts, dressed with sweet tamarind chutney and green chutney and finished with lemon and coriander. It's the Mumbai beach snack, and Kolkata's jhal muri and Karnataka's churmuri are close cousins.

Puffed rice is light by volume, so a plate looks bigger than its calories. The fried sev and papdi, and the peanuts, are where most of the fat comes from.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home bhel puri compare with outside?

Street bhel is mixed to order, and plates vary a lot, especially in how much sev goes on top. Some stalls add raw mango, boiled chana or more peanuts.

No lab has measured street bhel, so the range below runs from 0.9 to 1.4 times this plate.

What changes the calories in bhel puri?

  • Sev is the most calorie-dense part: 10 g is about 55 calories, so a heavy hand with it adds up.
  • Peanuts add about 25 calories per 5 g, and each papdi about 20.
  • Sweet chutney adds about 20 calories a tablespoon. Dry bhel without it is a little lighter.
  • More onion, tomato, cucumber or raw mango adds volume and very few calories.

How were these numbers worked out?

No standard recipe for this plate exists in the Indian Nutrient Databank, so the plate is assembled from typical amounts, each stated below and valued from IFCT 2017. The puffed rice and peanuts follow the proportions of the databank's open-source bhel puri (60 g puffed rice and 2 tablespoons of peanuts for 2 to 3 plates).

Sev valued as besan plus oil at 39.6% fat, the fat content Gupta et al. 2016 measured in bhujia (fried besan noodles) from a Delhi vendor. Papdi valued as maida plus oil at 38.5% fat, the fat content Gupta et al. 2016 measured in mathri (fried wheat crackers) from a Delhi vendor. 4 g per papdi is an assumption. Sweet chutney as The Art & Science of Cooking's saunth chutney (ASC281: 25 g tamarind and 50 g jaggery in 100 ml water), so 1 tablespoon (15 g) holds 2.1 g tamarind and 4.3 g jaggery, about 21 kcal.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 plate of bhel puri?

About 265 for a plate with 25 g of puffed rice, 10 g of sev and two papdis.

How many calories are in 100 g of bhel puri?

About 235 for this mix, chutneys included.

How much protein is in bhel puri?

About 6 g in a plate, from the puffed rice, sev and peanuts.

Which has more calories, bhel puri or sev puri?

A plate of sev puri is about 295, a little more than bhel at about 265, because it has more papdi and sev.
