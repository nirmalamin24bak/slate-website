Fish curry calories

A bowl of home fish curry with 100 g of fish has about 280 calories, and one 50 g piece with its gravy about 140.

Fish curry is cooked all along India's coasts and rivers: Bengali machher jhol with rohu or katla and mustard oil, Goan and Kerala curries with coconut and kokum or tamarind, and north Indian onion-tomato gravies. This page uses a textbook onion-tomato curry with rohu.

Rohu and similar river fish are lean, about 2% fat, so most of this curry's fat is the oil. Coconut-based curries add a lot more.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Restaurant fish curries are often richer, and Goan and Kerala curries are made with coconut milk or ground coconut, which adds a lot.

What changes the number

  • Oil is the biggest variable in a lean-fish curry. Each teaspoon adds about 40 calories.
  • Coconut milk adds about 140 calories per 60 g, and ground coconut more again.
  • Oily fish such as sardine have more fat than rohu. Per 100 g, IFCT 2017 puts rohu at 102 calories and sardine at about 150.
  • Frying the fish before adding it to the gravy adds the oil it takes up.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the fish curry in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g fish with onion, tomato, half a lemon, curd, ginger, garlic and spices, cooked in a tablespoon of oil. The textbook allows singhara, rohu or surmai; rohu is used, as the databank does. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Half a lemon is taken as 24 g of juice, from USDA's 48 g per lemon. The textbook adds no water, and katoris are worked out at 1 g per ml.

That works out to about 110 calories per 100 g of cooked fish curry. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

USDA's survey value for fish curry is about 93 calories per 100 g, and Basic Food Preparation's Bengali fish curry, made with pomfret and mustard oil, is about 340 a bowl. This page's bowl is about 280. Depending on the fish and the oil, a bowl with 100 g of fish is anywhere from about 235 to 340.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 piece of fish curry?

About 140 for a 50 g piece of rohu with its share of gravy.

How many calories are in 100 g of fish curry?

About 110 for this onion-tomato curry. USDA's survey value is 93.

How much protein is in fish curry?

About 22 g in a bowl with 100 g of rohu.

How many calories are in fish curry and rice?

A bowl of fish curry is about 280, and a 200 ml katori of plain rice adds about 160.
