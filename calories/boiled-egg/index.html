Boiled egg calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

One boiled egg, about 50 g without the shell, has about 65 calories, with 7 g of protein and 5 g of fat. Two boiled eggs are about 135 and three about 200. Boiling adds nothing: the number is the egg itself, so a bigger egg has more. ICMR-NIN counts a boiled egg at 90, which fits an egg of about 65 g.

A boiled egg is the simplest thing to count on this site: there is no oil, no masala and no cooked weight to guess. The only question is the size of the egg, and that is where the sources differ.

Indian eggs sold loose are often 45 to 55 g. The 50 g egg used here is the Indian Nutrient Databank's household measure and the same as USDA's large egg.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the calories in boiled egg?

  • Egg size. A 50 g egg has about 65 calories; an egg of 60 to 65 g has about a quarter more, which is where ICMR-NIN's 90 comes from.
  • The yolk holds nearly all the fat, so an egg eaten without its yolk has far fewer calories and about half the protein.
  • Boiling time changes nothing: soft-boiled and hard-boiled eggs have the same calories.
  • What goes with it does. A boiled egg in egg curry picks up the masala's oil, about 185 per egg with gravy.
  • Duck eggs and desi eggs are a little larger and richer than farm hen's eggs; the values here are for the ordinary white hen's egg.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the boiled egg in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): one hen's egg, which the databank counts as 50 g without the shell. Values are IFCT 2017's whole raw egg; boiling in the shell doesn't change the weight or the nutrients.

USDA's hard-boiled egg (155 calories per 100 g) and ICMR-NIN's boiled egg (90 calories) are shown as cross-checks, and the page states a range because they sit more than 15% above this value.

ICMR-NIN counts a boiled egg at 90 calories, and USDA's large egg works out to about 78, against 65 here from IFCT 2017's egg. Egg size explains most of it: a 50 g egg is a medium Indian egg, and a 60 to 65 g egg has a quarter more. A boiled egg is about 65 to 90 calories depending on its size.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 boiled egg?

About 65 calories for an egg of about 50 g without the shell, with 7 g of protein.

How many calories are in 2 boiled eggs?

About 135 calories, with 13 g of protein and 9 g of fat.

How many calories are in 3 boiled eggs?

About 200 calories, with 20 g of protein.

How much protein is in a boiled egg?

About 7 g in a 50 g egg, and about 13 g in 2 eggs. The white and the yolk each hold roughly half of it.

Which has more calories, a boiled egg or an omelette?

The omelette, because of the oil and the second egg: about 215 for a 2-egg omelette against 135 for 2 boiled eggs. A single-egg omelette is about 105.
