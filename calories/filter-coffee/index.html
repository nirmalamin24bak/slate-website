Filter coffee calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A tumbler of filter coffee, 50 ml of decoction with 100 ml of whole milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar, has about 105 calories. Without sugar it's about 75, and with toned milk and 2 teaspoons about 90. Sugar matters most, about 16 calories a level teaspoon. Darshini coffee, sweeter and sometimes bigger, runs about 105 to 145.

Filter coffee is a strong decoction, dripped slowly through a brass or steel filter from ground coffee and chicory, mixed with hot milk and sugar, and frothed by pouring between a tumbler and davara. It's the morning drink across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala.

The decoction itself has almost no calories. The milk and the sugar are the whole number.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home filter coffee compare with outside?

Filter coffee in a darshini or Udupi hotel is usually sweeter than home coffee, and sometimes a little bigger, made with full-cream milk.

No lab has measured it, so the range below allows up to a teaspoon more sugar and a serving up to 1.2 times as big.

What changes the calories in filter coffee?

  • Sugar is the main variable: each level teaspoon adds about 16 calories.
  • Toned milk instead of whole milk saves about 15 calories a tumbler; buffalo milk adds about 35.
  • More decoction and less milk lowers it; the decoction itself is close to zero.
  • Chicory in the coffee powder adds nothing measurable to the cup.

How were these numbers worked out?

One tumbler is taken as 50 ml of decoction and 100 ml of whole milk. ICMR-NIN counts a cup of coffee with 2 teaspoons of sugar and 100 ml (taken here as the milk) at 110 calories. Milk values are from IFCT 2017; coffee and sugar from USDA. A level teaspoon of sugar is 4.2 g (USDA).

The toned-milk row uses Amul Taaza's label, 3% fat, as on the masala chai page.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in filter coffee?

About 105 for a tumbler with whole milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar, and about 75 without sugar.

How many calories are in filter coffee with toned milk?

About 90 with toned milk and 2 teaspoons of sugar.

Which has more calories, filter coffee or chai?

Filter coffee, usually, because it has more milk. A cup of masala chai with 2 teaspoons of sugar is about 60.

How many calories are in black filter coffee?

About 1 calorie in 50 ml of decoction. Sugar adds about 16 per teaspoon.
