Butter naan calories

A home naan brushed with a teaspoon of butter has about 210 calories. A large restaurant butter naan comes to about 420 to 630, depending on size.

Butter naan is plain naan brushed with melted butter as it comes out of the tandoor. In restaurants and on delivery apps it's the most ordered bread with butter chicken, dal makhani and paneer dishes, and garlic butter naan adds chopped garlic and coriander to the same butter.

The butter is easy to see but it's the smaller part. The naan itself, and especially its size, decides most of the number.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

A restaurant butter naan is usually two to three times the size of a home naan, and the butter is spread over all of it. Some places add a second brush of butter before serving, and garlic butter naan often has more.

No lab has measured a restaurant butter naan, so the range below scales this naan and its teaspoon of butter up 2 to 3 times, in line with USDA's 177 g, 10-inch naan.

The outside range is a naan 2 to 3 times the size of the home one, with butter in proportion (2 to 3 teaspoons).

What changes the number

  • Each teaspoon of butter adds about 35 calories, and a teaspoon of ghee about 45.
  • Naan size counts for more than the butter. A large naan is about three times a home one before any butter goes on.
  • Garlic butter naan uses the same butter, plus garlic and coriander that add very little.
  • Sharing one large naan between two is about half of the outside range each.

How these numbers were worked out

The naan is the one in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank), with 80 g of maida for 2 naans. The textbook brushes 2 naans with a teaspoon of butter, which is the half-teaspoon row in the table. Butter values are from USDA, the rest from IFCT 2017.

Restaurant sizes follow USDA's survey data, where a 10-inch naan weighs 177 g.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 butter naan?

About 210 for a home naan with a teaspoon of butter. A large restaurant butter naan is about 595.

How many calories are in 2 butter naans?

Two home butter naans with a teaspoon of butter each come to about 420. Two large restaurant ones can pass 1,000.

How much more is butter naan than plain naan?

About 35 calories for each teaspoon of butter. A home naan is about 175 plain and about 210 with a teaspoon of butter.

How much protein is in a butter naan?

About 5 g in a home butter naan, and about 15 g in a large restaurant one. Butter adds almost none.
