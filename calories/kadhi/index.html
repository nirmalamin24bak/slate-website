Kadhi calories

A 200 ml katori of kadhi without pakoras has about 100 calories. A bowl of kadhi pakora, with its share of fried besan pakoras, has about 360.

Kadhi is curd whisked with besan, simmered until it thickens and tempered with mustard or cumin, methi seeds, dry red chillies and curry leaves. Punjabi kadhi is thick and comes with fried besan pakoras; Gujarati kadhi is thin, white and a little sweet, eaten with khichdi.

The kadhi itself is light: mostly water and curd. The tadka oil and, above all, the fried pakoras are where most of the calories in a bowl of kadhi pakora come from.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

At a dhaba, kadhi pakora is usually served with more pakoras in a bigger bowl, and the pakoras are fried in oil that's been used many times.

The pakoras on this page already use the fat content of street pakoras measured in a lab, so the range below only allows for a serving up to 1.3 times as big.

What changes the number

  • Pakoras are the biggest variable. A bowl's share of fried pakoras adds more than the kadhi itself: about 360 for the bowl with them, against 140 without.
  • The tadka oil is next: each teaspoon adds about 40 calories to the pot.
  • Thin Gujarati kadhi has less besan per katori and often a teaspoon of sugar, about 16 calories.
  • Full-fat curd and sour curd count about the same. Low-fat curd lowers it a little.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the besan kadhi with pakodies in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g curd, 20 g besan and 2 glasses of water for 2 bowls, with a tablespoon of oil in the tadka. Its pakoras use 30 g besan with spinach and onion. Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd and chilli powder from USDA.

The databank counts all the pakora frying oil as eaten. This page instead assumes the pakoras end up 30.6% fat, the figure a lab measured in street pakoras in Delhi. That's about 19 g of oil per bowl's share of pakoras.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in kadhi without pakora?

About 100 in a 200 ml katori, and about 140 in a bowl of half this recipe.

How many calories are in kadhi pakora?

About 360 for a bowl with its share of pakoras. The pakoras are most of it.

How many calories are in kadhi chawal?

Add a 200 ml katori of plain rice, about 160 calories, to the kadhi: 100 without pakoras or 360 for a bowl with them.

How much protein is in kadhi?

About 5 g in a bowl without pakoras, from the curd and besan, and about 8 g with them.
