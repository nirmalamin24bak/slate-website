Naan calories

A home naan (about 40 g maida) has about 175 calories. A large 10-inch restaurant naan, about 177 g, has about 525.

Naan is a soft, leavened maida bread, made with curd and milk and baked against the wall of a tandoor. At home it's usually cooked on a tawa and finished over the flame. It's the standard bread with dal makhani, paneer and chicken curries in north Indian restaurants.

Gram for gram, naan has a little more than roti, from the maida, milk and sugar. A restaurant naan has far more mostly because it's so much bigger.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

A tandoor naan from a restaurant is usually far bigger than one made at home. USDA's survey data puts a 10-inch naan at 177 g, about three times this home naan. Most are brushed with butter too, which is on the butter naan page.

What changes the number

  • Size is the biggest variable by far. Half a large restaurant naan has more than a whole home naan.
  • Butter or ghee brushed on top adds about 35 to 45 calories a teaspoon.
  • Garlic naan adds butter as well as garlic, and stuffed naans and kulchas carry their filling.
  • Whole-wheat naan is close per gram: USDA lists it at 286 calories per 100 g, against 291 for plain naan.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the naan in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of maida with curd, milk, a teaspoon of sugar and half a teaspoon of melted fat, making 2 to 3 naans. This page uses 2. The textbook's teaspoon of butter for brushing is left out here and counted on the butter naan page. Seeds and spices under 1 g are left out.

Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd and sugar from USDA. Cooked weight comes from water content: the dough's solids at the 29.2% water of USDA's naan.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 naan?

A home naan made with about 40 g of maida has about 175 calories. A large restaurant naan is about 525, because it's about three times the size.

Which has more calories, naan or roti?

A naan, mainly because it's bigger. Per 100 g naan has somewhat more too: about 295 calories, against about 240 for roti.

Is naan made of maida?

Usually, yes. Restaurant naan is almost always maida. Whole-wheat naan exists and has about the same calories per gram.

How much protein is in a naan?

About 5 g in a home naan, and about 15 g in a large restaurant naan.
