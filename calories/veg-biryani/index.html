Veg biryani calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

A plate of veg biryani (50 g of raw rice with vegetables, about 395 g cooked) has about 520 calories. 250 g is about 330.

Veg biryani is rice parboiled with whole spices and layered with a masala of vegetables, fried onion, curd and saffron milk, then cooked on dum. Hyderabadi, Lucknowi and Kolkata styles differ in spice and richness, and many homes make a quicker one-pot version.

This textbook biryani uses 4 tablespoons of oil for two plates, so oil is close to half of its calories. That's the number to watch.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home veg biryani compare with outside?

Restaurant and delivery biryani portions are often bigger than a home plate, and some are finished with ghee or fried onions on top.

What changes the calories in veg biryani?

  • Oil or ghee is the biggest variable. Each tablespoon across the pot adds about 120 calories, about 60 to each plate.
  • Fried onions (birista) take up oil. Shop-bought fried onions count as well.
  • More rice and fewer vegetables raises the number per plate; more vegetables lowers it.
  • Raita and salan are separate. A katori of raita made with curd is about 60 to 90 calories.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the vegetable biryani in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g rice, 300 g of potato, peas, carrot, cauliflower, beans and tomato, 100 g onion, curd, saffron milk and 4 tablespoons of oil, for 2 plates. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The rice is taken at 2.98 times its raw weight once cooked, as on the plain rice page, and katoris use USDA's 172 g per cup for biryani with vegetables.

That works out to about 130 calories per 100 g of cooked veg biryani. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

USDA's survey value for vegetable biryani is about 109 calories per 100 g, below this page's 130, because it uses less oil. Home biryani made with less oil can be about a fifth lower than the figures here.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 plate of veg biryani?

About 520 for a home plate made from 50 g of raw rice with vegetables, about 395 g cooked.

How many calories are in 250 g of veg biryani?

About 330, and about 655 for 500 g.

How much protein is in veg biryani?

About 9 g in a plate, from the rice, peas and curd.

Which has more calories, veg biryani or chicken biryani?

It depends on the oil more than the chicken. In these two textbook recipes the veg biryani uses far more oil, so a plate is about 520 against about 385 for chicken biryani.
