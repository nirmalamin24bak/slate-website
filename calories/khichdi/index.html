Khichdi calories

A 200 ml katori of moong dal khichdi has about 240 calories, with its share of a teaspoon of ghee. An extra teaspoon of ghee on top adds about 45.

Khichdi is rice and moong dal cooked together until soft, with ginger, jeera and a little ghee. It's the comfort food of most Indian homes: Gujarati khichdi with kadhi, Bengali khichuri at pujas, and Tamil ven pongal with pepper and ghee are all versions of it.

Rice is about two thirds of khichdi's calories here. How much water it's cooked with changes what a katori holds, and the ghee on top is the other big variable.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the number

  • Ghee on top is the biggest variable. Each teaspoon adds about 45 calories.
  • A soft, porridge-like khichdi with more water has fewer calories per katori; the pot's total stays the same.
  • More dal and less rice changes the calories very little, since dal and rice are close per gram, but it adds protein.
  • Vegetable khichdi with peas, carrot and potato adds a little. Kadhi on the side is counted separately: see kadhi.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the plain khichdi in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g rice and 20 g moong dal cooked in 2 cups of water with ginger, jeera and 5 g of ghee. Values are from IFCT 2017.

The databank adds all the water to the cooked weight, which makes a very thin khichdi. This page instead uses the water content of USDA's rice cooked with lentils, 67%, which is a soft khichdi with the water absorbed. Katoris use USDA's 207 g per cup.

That works out to about 135 calories per 100 g of cooked khichdi. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

ICMR-NIN counts a cup of khichdi at 200 calories, a little under this page's 240. Khichdi ranges from fluffy to porridge-thin, so a 200 ml katori can be anywhere from about 200 to 240, and less again if it's very watery.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 katori of khichdi?

About 240 for a 200 ml katori of soft khichdi, and about 180 for a 150 ml one.

How many calories are in khichdi with ghee?

About 285 for a 200 ml katori with an extra teaspoon of ghee on top.

How much protein is in khichdi?

About 7 g in a 200 ml katori, and about 10 g in a plate of the whole recipe.

How many calories are in 100 g of khichdi?

About 135 for this soft khichdi.

Which has more calories, khichdi or rice?

Khichdi, per katori: about 240 against about 160 for a 200 ml katori of plain rice. Soft khichdi packs more into a katori than fluffy rice, and it has ghee.
