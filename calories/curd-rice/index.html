Curd rice calories

A 200 ml katori of curd rice has about 220 calories. A plate made from 80 g of raw rice with curd, milk and a tadka is about 410.

Curd rice is soft-cooked rice mashed with curd and a little milk, tempered with mustard seeds, dry red chilli, curry leaves, ginger and green chilli. It ends most meals in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra, and it's a common tiffin and travel food because the milk keeps it from turning sour too fast.

Rice is about two thirds of its calories. Curd and milk add about a fifth, and the tadka oil about a tenth.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Curd rice in a meals hotel or restaurant is much the same dish. It's sometimes richer, with cream or more milk, tempered in ghee and topped with pomegranate or grapes.

No lab has measured it, so the range below allows a katori up to 1.3 times as dense and up to a teaspoon of ghee.

What changes the number

  • The ratio of rice to curd matters most. More curd and less rice lowers the calories per katori.
  • Full-fat curd and milk add more than toned. Cream, as some restaurants add, adds about 30 calories a tablespoon.
  • Without the tadka a plate is about 40 calories lighter.
  • Pomegranate, grated carrot and cucumber add very little.

How these numbers were worked out

The recipe is the curd rice in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g rice with a quarter cup each of curd and milk, tempered with a teaspoon of oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves, dry red chilli, ginger and green chilli. Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd from USDA.

The rice is taken at 2.98 times its raw weight once cooked, as on the plain rice page. Mashed curd rice is taken as 1 g per ml.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 bowl of curd rice?

About 220 in a 200 ml katori and about 275 in a 250 ml bowl. A plate made from 80 g of raw rice is about 410.

How many calories are in 100 g of curd rice?

About 110.

How much protein is in curd rice?

About 6 g in a 200 ml katori, from the rice, curd and milk.

How many calories are in curd rice without tadka?

About 40 fewer for the plate: the tadka's teaspoon of oil is the main part of it.
