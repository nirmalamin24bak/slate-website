Samosa calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

A home aloo samosa has about 165 calories. A street samosa is usually 160 to 285, depending on its size and how much oil it took up.

A samosa is a maida pastry, kneaded with ghee or oil, filled with spiced potato and peas, and deep-fried. Punjabi samosas are large with a thick, ajwain-spiced crust; Bengal's singara is smaller.

Most of a samosa's calories come from oil: some in the dough, and more taken up in the kadhai. The potato and pea filling adds comparatively little.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home samosa compare with outside?

A peer-reviewed study weighed and tested street samosas from vendors in Delhi and a nearby village. They held about 29 g of fat per 100 g, against about 18 g in a fresh home samosa. Oil that's used again and again, and a thicker crust, are common reasons.

The range below uses those measured samosas: a small one of about 47 g and a large one of about 79 g.

What changes the calories in samosa?

  • Oil taken up in frying is the biggest single part of a samosa's calories. Frying at the right heat and draining well keeps it lower.
  • More ghee or oil in the dough makes a flakier crust and adds about 45 calories per teaspoon to the batch.
  • A large Punjabi samosa can be half as big again as a small one.
  • ICMR-NIN counts a tablespoon of tamarind chutney with jaggery at about 60 calories. Samosa chaat with curd, chole and sev is a meal of its own.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the potato samosa in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 60 g maida with 2 teaspoons of ghee, 150 g potato, 75 g peas and 12 g of oil for the filling, making 6 samosas. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Frying oil is harder to count. The databank counts all of it as eaten, which gives far too high a number. This page assumes a fresh home samosa ends up about 18% fat, in line with lab-fried samosas (18.3%) and USDA's samosa recipe (17.5%). That works out to about 8 g of oil taken up per samosa.

Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

ICMR-NIN counts one samosa at 200 calories, a little above this page's 165. Street samosas are measured as fattier, so a samosa can reasonably be anywhere from about 165 to 285 calories.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 samosa?

A home aloo samosa has about 165 calories. A street samosa is usually 160 to 285, depending on its size and the oil.

How much fat is in a samosa?

A home samosa has about 12 g of fat. Street samosas measured in a Delhi study had about 14 to 23 g each.

Do baked or air-fried samosas have fewer calories?

Yes, because they don't take up frying oil. The crust and filling stay the same, so the difference is roughly the oil, about 8 g in a home samosa here, or about 70 calories.

How many calories are in samosa with chutney?

Add the chutney: ICMR-NIN counts a tablespoon of tamarind chutney with jaggery at about 60 calories, and a tablespoon of green chutney at very little.
