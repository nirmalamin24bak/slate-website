Dal fry calories

A 200 ml katori of home dal fry has about 180 calories. It's thicker than everyday dal tadka and cooked into an onion and tomato masala, so a katori holds more dal.

Dal fry is toor dal, cooked soft and then stirred into onion, tomato, green chilli and spices fried in ghee, with a jeera tempering. In dhabas and on restaurant menus it sits next to dal tadka; the difference is that the masala is cooked into the dal rather than poured over at the end, and dal fry is usually thicker.

Most of the calories come from two things in roughly equal parts: the dal and the ghee.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Dhaba dal fry is often thicker than home dal fry and finished with an extra spoon of ghee or butter, sometimes a second tadka with dry red chillies.

What changes the number

  • Each teaspoon of ghee or oil in the masala or the tempering adds about 45 calories to the pot.
  • Thickness decides how much dal is in a katori. This dal fry is about half water by weight; a runnier one has fewer calories per katori.
  • Onion and tomato add little: under 15 calories for the whole bowl here.
  • Dal fry made with moong or masoor is within a few percent of toor per gram in IFCT 2017, so the fat and thickness matter more than the dal.

How these numbers were worked out

No standard textbook has a single dal fry recipe, so this one combines three from Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 30 g of toor dal as in its arhar dal, its onion-tomato masala (20 g onion, 15 g tomato, 5 g ghee) and its jeera tempering (5 g ghee). Values are from IFCT 2017.

The water is half that of the dal tadka page, for a thicker dal. That's an assumption, and the range below shows what thickness does.

That works out to about 90 calories per 100 g of cooked dal fry. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5 and grams to the nearest whole number.

Dal thickness varies more than anything else here. ICMR-NIN counts half a cup of plain dal at 100 calories, close to this page's 90, while USDA's survey value for dal is about 145 per 100 g. A 200 ml katori of dal fry can be anywhere from about 180 to 290, depending on how thick it is.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 bowl of dal fry?

A bowl made from 30 g of dry toor dal with 10 g of ghee has about 205 calories. A 200 ml katori is about 180.

What's the difference between dal fry and dal tadka?

In dal fry the onion-tomato masala is cooked into the dal; in dal tadka a ghee tempering is poured on at the end. Dal fry is usually thicker, so a 200 ml katori is about 180 calories here against about 115 for dal tadka.

How much protein is in dal fry?

About 6 g in a 200 ml katori, and about 7 g in a bowl made from 30 g of dry dal.

How many calories are in 100 g of dal fry?

About 90 calories per 100 g of this dal fry. A thinner dal has less, a thicker one more.

How many calories are in dal fry and rice?

A 200 ml katori of dal fry is about 180, and a 200 ml katori of plain rice about 160.
