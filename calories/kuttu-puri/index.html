Kuttu puri calories

One kuttu puri (about 13 g of kuttu atta with a little potato, deep-fried) has about 105 calories. Four have about 420.

Kuttu puri is made from buckwheat flour, which has no gluten, so the dough is bound with boiled potato and rolled out between sheets before deep-frying. It's eaten on Navratri and other fasting days with aloo sabzi and curd, when wheat and rice are avoided.

Kuttu flour has about the same calories per gram as wheat atta. As with any puri, the oil it takes up in frying is about half its calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

Home or outside

Kuttu puris from a sweet shop or a Navratri thali are often a little bigger, and fried in oil that's been used many times.

A lab study found fried foods took up about a fifth more fat after 32 fryings in the same oil. The range below uses that, with puris up to 1.3 times this size.

What changes the number

  • As with any puri, size and frying oil decide the number.
  • Kuttu flour is close to wheat atta per gram: USDA puts it at 335 calories per 100 g, against 320 for atta in IFCT 2017.
  • Singhara (water chestnut) and rajgira (amaranth) puris are similar per gram.
  • Aloo sabzi and curd with it are counted separately.

How these numbers were worked out

No standard textbook in the Indian Nutrient Databank has kuttu puri, so this follows the textbook puri on the puri page with kuttu atta in place of wheat, bound with 40 g of boiled potato (an assumption, since buckwheat has no gluten). Kuttu values are from USDA's buckwheat flour; potato and oil from IFCT 2017.

As on the puri page, the oil taken up is set so the puri matches USDA's puri composition, 24.9% fat, which is about 6 g per puri.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 kuttu puri?

About 105 for a home kuttu puri made with about 13 g of flour.

How many calories are in 4 kuttu puris?

About 420 for 4, and about 580 with a 200 ml katori of aloo sabzi cooked in ghee.

Is a kuttu puri lighter than a wheat puri?

No, about the same. A home puri of a similar size is about 95; the oil decides most of the difference.

How much protein is in a kuttu puri?

About 2 g.
