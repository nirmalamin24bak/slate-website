Peda calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 15 October 2026.

One peda of about 28 g, khoa cooked with sugar, has about 120 calories; 2 about 235. A small packed peda of 13 g is about 55. Khoa is two thirds of the calories and sugar nearly all the rest. Every sweet shop makes it the same way, so only the size moves the number: 100 g is about 425.

Peda is khoa, milk reduced to solids, cooked with sugar until it holds together, then pressed into a disc and stamped. Mathura's is caramelised brown, Dharwad's darker still, and the plain white doodh peda is the one in most sweet boxes. It is the simplest of the khoa sweets, with burfi and milk cake as close relations.

Because it is only khoa and sugar, the recipe hardly varies between homes and shops. A lab survey of market pedas and a packed label both land within 5% of this page per gram.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

What changes the calories in peda?

  • Size is the only real variable: packed pedas run about 13 g, a halwai's 20 to 30 g, a Mathura peda up to 35 g.
  • Sugar share: market pedas run from 32% to 62% sugar. Sugar and khoa are close per gram (387 and 316 calories per 100 g), so a sweeter peda is a few percent more, not a lot.
  • Malai or kesar peda made with cream or extra ghee is a little richer; a drier, longer-cooked Mathura peda a little more per gram because there is less water.
  • Kaju katli is richer per gram, about 505 per 100 g, but its pieces are smaller.
  • Pista or silver leaf on top adds nothing you can measure.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the plain burfi in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 100 g of khoa cooked with 35 g of castor sugar and 2 teaspoons of milk, with 2 almonds on top, making 4 pieces. Peda and plain khoa burfi are the same mixture shaped differently, so the burfi recipe is used for peda. Values are from IFCT 2017, with sugar from USDA. The 2 tablespoons of ghee the textbook uses to grease the tray are not counted.

Khoa is 42.5% water in IFCT 2017 and loses more as the peda cooks. The finished weight is set at the 13.6% moisture that a National Dairy Research Institute survey measured in market pedas, which also puts this recipe at 18.6% fat, 15% protein and 31.5% sugar, close to the survey's 16.7%, 15.2% and 34.4%. The result is within 5% of the survey's composition and of a packed peda label.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 peda?

About 120 for a 28 g peda, and about 55 for a small 13 g packed one.

How many calories are in 2 pedas?

About 235 for two 28 g pedas.

How many calories are in 100 g of peda?

About 425. A packed label (Rajhans) lists 442, and a lab survey of market pedas works out to about 419.

How much sugar is in a peda?

About 9 g of added sugar in a 28 g peda, plus about 4 g of the milk's own sugar from the khoa.

Which has more calories, peda or kaju katli?

Per piece the peda, about 120 against about 50 for a 10 g sweet-shop kaju katli. Per 100 g the kaju katli, about 505 against 425, because cashews are richer than khoa.
