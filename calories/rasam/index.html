Rasam calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

A 200 ml katori of tomato rasam has about 50 calories, and a 150 ml tumbler to drink about 40. Rasam rice, with a 200 ml katori of rice, is about 210. The tadka is nearly all of it: a teaspoon of ghee or oil adds about 40 to 45. Restaurant rasam runs about 50 to 95.

Rasam is a thin, peppery soup of tamarind and tomato, with a little cooked toor dal and a spice powder of coriander, cumin, pepper and dry red chilli, tempered with mustard, garlic and curry leaves. It's served with rice in Tamil, Andhra and Karnataka meals, and drunk on its own from a tumbler.

It's one of the lightest things in a south Indian meal. Most of the calories in a katori come from the tadka oil and the small amount of dal.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home rasam compare with outside?

Rasam in a meals hotel or restaurant is much like home rasam. Some places temper it with ghee, and it's usually refilled freely.

With no lab figure to go on, the range below adds up to a teaspoon of ghee to the tadka for each katori.

What changes the calories in rasam?

  • The tadka is most of a katori's calories. A teaspoon of ghee or oil adds about 40 to 45.
  • Dal rasam with more toor dal has more: each extra 10 g of dal adds about 35 calories to the pot.
  • Lemon rasam and pepper rasam are close to tomato rasam per katori.
  • Rice with rasam usually counts for far more than the rasam itself.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the tamarind rasam in Basic Food Preparation, a standard Indian cookery manual (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 10 g toor dal, 50 g tomato, tamarind, dry and green chilli, ginger, coriander seed and pepper, in 1.25 cups of water with half a teaspoon of oil. Values are from IFCT 2017.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 cup of rasam?

About 50 in a 200 ml katori, and about 40 in a 150 ml tumbler.

How many calories are in rasam rice?

About 210 for a 200 ml katori of rasam with a 200 ml katori of plain rice. Most of that is the rice.

How much protein is in rasam?

About 2 g in a 200 ml katori. Rasam has little dal, so it's not a large source.

How many calories are in 100 g of rasam?

About 25.
