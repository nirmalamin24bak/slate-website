Pav bhaji calories

Checked on 13 October 2026.

A plate of pav bhaji with 2 butter-toasted pav has about 550 calories. The bhaji alone is about 215, and each butter pav adds about 170.

Pav bhaji is a thick, spiced mash of potato, peas, tomato, cauliflower, capsicum and carrot, cooked on a big tawa with butter and pav bhaji masala, served with soft pav toasted in more butter, chopped onion and lemon. It started as a quick meal for Mumbai's mill workers and is now sold everywhere.

Butter is the key number. In this recipe the butter in the bhaji and on the pav is close to half of a plate's calories.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home pav bhaji compare with outside?

Street pav bhaji is known for its butter: a cube melted into each plate's bhaji on the tawa, and pav toasted in more of it. Amul-style butter pav bhaji can carry a lot more than this home plate.

No lab has measured it, so the range below adds 10 to 25 g more butter to one plate with 2 pav.

What changes the calories in pav bhaji?

  • Butter is the biggest variable. Each teaspoon adds about 35 calories, and street plates often have several more.
  • Each extra butter pav adds about 170. A plain pav without butter is about 135.
  • More potato in the bhaji raises it a little; more cauliflower, capsicum or peas changes it little.
  • Without butter, a plate of bhaji is about 95 calories lighter.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the pav bhaji in the Indian Nutrient Databank's open recipe set: potato, tomato, peas, carrot, cauliflower, capsicum, beans and onion with pav bhaji masala, cooked with 3 tablespoons of butter, for 5 plates, with 12 pav and 5 to 7 more tablespoons of butter for topping and toasting. This page uses 6 tablespoons: a teaspoon for each pav and the rest on top of the bhaji. Values are from IFCT 2017, with butter and pav from USDA.

Whole spices that aren't eaten, and those under 1 g, are left out. Each pav is 43 g, as in the recipe (12 pav weighing 516 g).

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 plate of pav bhaji?

About 550 for a plate of bhaji with 2 butter pav, and about 890 with 4 pav.

How many calories are in pav bhaji without pav?

About 215 for a plate of the bhaji alone.

How many calories are in pav bhaji without butter?

Leaving out all the butter takes off about 95 calories from the bhaji and about 35 from each pav, so a plate with 2 plain pav is about 385.

How much protein is in pav bhaji?

About 15 g in a plate with 2 pav, mostly from the pav and the peas.
