Bhatura calories

By Nirmal Amin. Checked on 14 October 2026.

One bhatura from about 27 g of maida, deep-fried, has about 185 calories, and 2 bhature about 370. With a katori of chole, 2 bhature make a plate of about 635. Size matters most: a bhatura from 40 g of maida has half as much again. Street bhaturas tested in a lab came to about 90 to 185.

Bhatura is a leavened maida bread, kneaded with curd and sometimes a little baking powder, then deep-fried until it puffs into a large round. It's almost always eaten as chole bhature, a Punjabi dish found from Delhi's street stalls to restaurant breakfasts.

A bhatura takes up a lot of oil in frying. In this page's numbers, oil is close to half its calories, and the maida most of the rest.

Typical values for a home kitchen, for the portion named next to each number. Other sites often show bigger numbers because they count a bigger serving or a restaurant recipe. Your own katori, roti and oil change the number; Slate adjusts for them.

How does home bhatura compare with outside?

A peer-reviewed study bought bhaturas from street vendors in Delhi and a nearby village and tested them in a lab. The Delhi bhatura weighed 52 g with 10.2 g of fat, much like the home bhatura here. The village one was smaller, 33 g, and held much less fat.

Restaurant bhaturas for chole bhature can be bigger than either. We found no measurement of those, so the range below covers the two street bhaturas only.

What changes the calories in bhatura?

  • Size is the biggest variable. A bhatura rolled from 40 g of maida has half as much again as this one.
  • Oil uptake depends on the heat and the oil. A bhatura fried in cooler or much-used oil soaks up more.
  • Curd and a pinch of sugar in the dough add very little, about 5 calories a bhatura.
  • A 200 ml katori of chole is a separate dish, and a fried green chilli and onion on the side add a little more.

How were these numbers worked out?

The recipe is the bhatura in The Art & Science of Cooking, a standard Indian cookery textbook (via the Indian Nutrient Databank): 80 g of maida and 20 g of curd with a pinch of sugar, making 2 to 3 bhaturas. This page uses 3. Values are from IFCT 2017, with curd and sugar from USDA.

The databank counts all the frying oil as eaten, which is far too high. No lab has measured a home bhatura, so this page takes the fat content of the bhatura measured from a Delhi vendor, 19.6%, as typical. That's about 10 g of oil taken up per bhatura.

Questions people ask

How many calories are in 1 bhatura?

About 185 for a bhatura made with about 27 g of maida and fried in fresh oil. Half a bhatura is about 90.

How many calories are in 2 bhature?

About 370 for two, before the chole.

Is bhatura made of maida?

Usually, yes. The textbook bhatura is maida with curd. Some homes mix in atta or semolina, which changes the number a little.

How much fat is in a bhatura?

About 10 g in this home bhatura. Street bhaturas tested in a lab held between 3.3 g and 10.2 g, depending on size and the oil.

How many calories are in chole bhature?

Two bhature with a 200 ml katori of chole come to about 635 calories, with about 15 g of protein.
