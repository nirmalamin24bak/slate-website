Slate and Lose It!

Checked on 11 October 2026.

Lose It! is a calorie counter with a large international food database, a free basic version with ads and community features, and Premium features such as barcode, photo and voice logging, on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and the web. Slate is a calorie journal for iPhone built around Indian home food, with no login and no ads.

Where Slate is different

  • No login. There's no account to create and no email or phone number to give; Slate creates an anonymous account on your phone.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking, no coaches and no sales calls. Your Slate is stored in Mumbai, India, and you can export or delete all of it from Settings.
  • Built around Indian kitchens. Home dishes are worked out from recipes in grams with IFCT 2017 values, then adjusted for your katori (150, 200 or 250 ml), your roti and your oil. Tap any number to see how it was worked out.
  • Written the way you'd say it: "2 roti aur ek katori dal", "kam tel", "bina cheeni". If Slate can't recognise a line it counts it as zero rather than guessing.
  • Free forever, with a public promise that nothing free today moves behind Plus. Slate never suggests a goal.

Where Lose It! is a better fit

  • You use Android, the web or an Apple Watch. Slate is iPhone only.
  • You'd like friends and groups for company. Lose It! includes community support in its free version.
  • You'd prefer to pay once. Lose It! sells a one-time ad removal and a lifetime Premium option.
  • You eat a lot of foods from outside India, which its large international database covers.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between Slate and Lose It!?

Lose It! is an international calorie counter with ads in its free version, a large food database and apps on many platforms. Slate is an iPhone calorie journal built around Indian home food, with no login and no ads.

Does Lose It! have Indian food?

Its database is international and you can create custom foods, but we found no India-specific claim on its official pages.

Does Slate have a lifetime plan?

No. Slate is free forever, and Slate Plus is ₹399 a month or ₹2,999 a year, with 7 days free on yearly.
