Slate and MyFitnessPal

Checked on 11 October 2026.

MyFitnessPal is a calorie counter with a very large global food database, including restaurant chains, free logging by search, and Premium features such as barcode, photo and voice logging, on iPhone, Android, Apple Watch and the web. Slate is a calorie journal for iPhone, built around Indian home food, with no login and no ads.

Where Slate is different

  • No login. There's no account to create and no email or phone number to give; Slate creates an anonymous account on your phone.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking, no coaches and no sales calls. Your Slate is stored in Mumbai, India, and you can export or delete all of it from Settings.
  • Built around Indian kitchens. Home dishes are worked out from recipes in grams with IFCT 2017 values, then adjusted for your katori (150, 200 or 250 ml), your roti and your oil. Tap any number to see how it was worked out.
  • Written the way you'd say it: "2 roti aur ek katori dal", "kam tel", "bina cheeni". If Slate can't recognise a line it counts it as zero rather than guessing.
  • Free forever, with a public promise that nothing free today moves behind Plus. Slate never suggests a goal.

Where MyFitnessPal is a better fit

  • You eat a lot of packaged, branded or chain-restaurant food. MyFitnessPal's database covers millions of foods, including restaurant chains.
  • You use Android, the web or a Wear OS watch. Slate is iPhone only.
  • You want free logging by search on several devices, including a computer.
  • You like a community. MyFitnessPal has forums and lets you share your diary with friends.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between Slate and MyFitnessPal?

MyFitnessPal is a global calorie counter with a very large food database, ads in its free version and an account to sign up for, on many platforms. Slate is an iPhone calorie journal built around Indian home food, with no login and no ads.

Does MyFitnessPal have Indian food?

Its database is global and users can add their own foods, but we found no India-specific claim on its official pages. Slate works out Indian dishes from recipes in grams with IFCT 2017 values.

How much is MyFitnessPal Premium in India?

On 11 October 2026 its Premium page, loaded from India, showed ₹619 a month or ₹3,099 a year. Prices can change, so check its page.
