Slate and HealthifyMe

Checked on 11 October 2026.

HealthifyMe is an Indian health app with photo and voice food logging, an Indian food database, an AI assistant and paid one-on-one coaching with nutritionists and dietitians, on iPhone and Android. Slate is a calorie journal for iPhone with no login, no ads and no coaching, where you type what you ate and each number is worked out from a recipe in grams and adjusted for your own kitchen.

Where Slate is different

  • No login. There's no account to create and no email or phone number to give; Slate creates an anonymous account on your phone.
  • No ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking, no coaches and no sales calls. Your Slate is stored in Mumbai, India, and you can export or delete all of it from Settings.
  • Built around Indian kitchens. Home dishes are worked out from recipes in grams with IFCT 2017 values, then adjusted for your katori (150, 200 or 250 ml), your roti and your oil. Tap any number to see how it was worked out.
  • Written the way you'd say it: "2 roti aur ek katori dal", "kam tel", "bina cheeni". If Slate can't recognise a line it counts it as zero rather than guessing.
  • Free forever, with a public promise that nothing free today moves behind Plus. Slate never suggests a goal.

Where HealthifyMe is a better fit

  • You want a person to guide you. HealthifyMe offers one-on-one coaching with nutritionists and dietitians; Slate has no coaching.
  • You use Android. HealthifyMe is on Android and iPhone; Slate is iPhone only.
  • You'd rather log by photo than by typing. HealthifyMe's Snap logs meals from photos, and can log automatically from your gallery.
  • You want a broader health app, with an AI assistant and paid plans, rather than a journal that leaves goals to you.

Questions people ask

What's the difference between Slate and HealthifyMe?

HealthifyMe is a health app with photo logging, an AI assistant and paid human coaching, on iPhone and Android, and it needs an account. Slate is a calorie journal for iPhone with no login, no ads and no coaching, where each number is worked out from a recipe and adjusted for your katori, roti and oil.

Does Slate have coaching like HealthifyMe?

No. Slate has no coaches and no sales calls, and it never suggests a goal. If you want a nutritionist or dietitian, an app with coaching, or a dietitian you see in person, is a better fit.

Is Slate free?

Yes. Slate is free forever. Slate Plus is optional, at ₹399 a month or ₹2,999 a year, with 7 days free on yearly.
