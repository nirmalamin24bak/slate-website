About Slate

Updated 13 October 2026.

Slate is a calorie journal for iPhone, built for Indian food by Sonal Systems Private Limited, a company in Vadodara, Gujarat. You type what you ate the way you'd say it, and Slate puts a number next to it.

Who makes Slate?

Slate is made by Sonal Systems Private Limited, founded in 2026 by Nirmal Amin and Nehal Modi. Nirmal writes the articles on this site.

Sonal Systems Private Limited is registered in India (CIN U62099GJ2026PTC179785). The office is at A-3, Mehsana Nagar, Nizampura, Vadodara 390002, Gujarat.

What is Slate built on?

  • Calories come from home recipes in grams and the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017). How Slate counts calories shows the arithmetic.
  • AI only reads lines Slate doesn't recognise, spots dishes in photos and powers chat. It never produces a calorie number, and your Slate isn't used to train AI models.
  • There's no login, and your data is stored in Mumbai, India. The privacy policy has the details.
  • Slate is free forever. Slate Plus is optional, at ₹399 a month or ₹2,999 a year.
  • Slate is not medical advice, and it's for people 18 or older.

How do I reach the people behind Slate?

Write to support@writeonslate.com for help, feedback or questions about your data. Support answers the common questions.
