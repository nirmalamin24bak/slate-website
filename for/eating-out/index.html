Calories in restaurant, Swiggy and Zomato food

Restaurant and delivery food usually has more calories than the same dish at home, mostly because of more butter, cream and oil and bigger portions. The honest way to count it is as a range, dish by dish.

Why outside food has more

Restaurant kitchens cook for taste and for portions that look generous. That usually means more ghee, butter and cream than a home kitchen uses, oil that's been used many times for frying, and bigger servings. None of it is printed on a menu, so the same dish can vary a lot from one place to the next.

Lab studies of street food in Delhi found samosas and kachoris with far more fat than home versions. A home samosa is about 165 calories; the street samosas measured came to about 160 to 285.

How to count an order

  1. Count each dish as its own line: the curry, the naans, the rice, the raita.
  2. Use the restaurant range, not the home number. If you don't know, the middle of the range is a fair guess.
  3. Count breads by the piece. A large restaurant naan is about three times a home naan before any butter goes on.
  4. If you share, count your share: half a plate of biryani is half the number.
  5. Don't forget drinks and sides: a sweet lassi, a gulab jamun, papad.

Questions people ask

Why does restaurant food have more calories than home food?

Mostly more butter, cream and oil, and bigger portions. Fried food from shops is often fried in oil that's used many times, which leaves more fat in each piece.

How many calories are in a Swiggy biryani?

No app can know a specific restaurant's recipe. As a range, a restaurant plate of chicken biryani is about 425 to 815 calories, depending on the ghee and the size.

How many calories are in a restaurant naan?

A large plain restaurant naan is about 525, and a large butter naan about 595.

Can Slate read my Swiggy or Zomato order?

Yes, with Slate Plus. Share or paste the order to Slate, check the list, and each dish is added as outside food. Slate only reads what you share and never connects to those apps.
