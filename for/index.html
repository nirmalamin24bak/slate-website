Who Slate is for

Slate is a calorie journal for people who eat Indian food: home dal and roti, a canteen thali, a Swiggy order and chai, often all in the same week. These pages explain how to count each of them honestly, and where Slate helps.

Every number on these pages comes from the food pages in Calories in Indian food, which show their recipes, arithmetic and sources.
